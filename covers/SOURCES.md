## Notes from the curation pass (2026-09-07)

Every cover was looked at before it shipped. Three file names lied, in both directions:
`WildArms.png` on the series article was the logo (replaced from the game article's
`Wildarmscase.jpg`); `BDFF_Logo.jpg` is the Nintendo 3DS box, and `Deluxe_package.jpg` is
the red GBA box, so both stayed. `Persona 5 Royal` shares the base game's Wikipedia
article, so its cover is *Persona 5*'s; `Dragon Quest V`'s article leads with the PS2
box; CrossCode's cover art is landscape by design.

Games Wikipedia gave no usable cover for: none.
