1. Introduction                                                       [INT01]
=============================================================================

This guide covers every relic and power-up in Lantern Vale. Nothing in it is
from a real game; it exists to exercise the heading detector.

To use it: 1. Go to the town. Then read the section you need.

===== Power-Ups =====

Each power-up raises one stat permanently. There are eight in the base game
and four more behind the post-game door.

POWER-UP LIST

FLAME CORE
Effect: +5 attack

FROST CORE
Effect: +5 defense

GALE CORE
Effect: +5 speed

STONE CORE
Effect: +5 guard

TIDE CORE
Effect: +5 magic

STAR CORE
Effect: +5 luck

[REL01] Relics
==============

Relics are found in the field and never sold. The author asks that this text
is not:
  1) used for nothing here
  2) given away as a product

4.2 Hidden Relics

Cross the bridge at night to find the Moon Relic behind the mill. The Relic
of Echoes sits under the third lantern.

SECRETS

The blue door opens once the lantern count reaches 12. You receive the Sun
Sigil after 30 wins in the harbor arena, and a second Relic after 50 wins.

Shops
-----
The vendor in Harbor sells Tide Cores for 250 coins each after chapter 3.
The exchange booth trades 10 shells for one Glow Bead.
