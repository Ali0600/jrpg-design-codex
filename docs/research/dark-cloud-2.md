### M166 Georama (town-building)
pointers: [wiki:darkcloud.fandom.com/Georama parts (Dark Chronicle)] [gf:36910 §Georama Requirements (GDREQUIRE), Sky_Render v1.20] [gf:53845 §8.1 Sindain Region 100% needs (Sin100%), Bahamut4ever v1.75]
row: M166
- Georama parts are built from crafting materials, and a part's blueprint must first be found in a Geostone [wiki:darkcloud.fandom.com/Georama parts (Dark Chronicle)] [gf:53845 §2. MATERIALS (geomat), Bahamut4ever v1.75]
- Geostones turn up on dungeon floors, each carrying a few parts (a Wooden House, a River, trees) [gf:36910 §This is a Geostone? (C2GEOSTON), Sky_Render v1.20] [gf:53845 §9. Special: Moon Flower Palace (specMFP), Bahamut4ever v1.75]
- The parts are houses, attachments for them, decorations and terrain such as rivers, hills and trees [wiki:darkcloud.fandom.com/Georama parts (Dark Chronicle)] [gf:53845 §3. HOUSES (and people) (geohou), Bahamut4ever v1.75]
- Most residents want something before they will move in; Gordon wants his house next to a tree and Aunt Polly a cart outside hers [wiki:darkcloud.fandom.com/Georama parts (Dark Chronicle)] [gf:36910 §A Stream and a Starlight Stair 10%, Sky_Render v1.20]
- Each town's rebuild is a list of restorations worth a percentage each, toward 100%: a lake restored by fifteen river parts, a shop reopened once its keeper lives there and the town has enough Culture Points [gf:36910 §Georama Requirements (GDREQUIRE), Sky_Render v1.20] [gf:53845 §8.1 Sindain Region 100% needs (Sin100%), Bahamut4ever v1.75]
- Georama is built in the present to restore the town's future, which Max then visits [wiki:darkcloud.fandom.com/Georama parts (Dark Chronicle)] [gf:36910 §15 River (12 entries), Sky_Render v1.20]
- Completing a town's Georama pays a weapon: the Jurak Gun for Sindain, the Serpent Slicer for Balance Valley [gf:36910 §Georama Requirements (GDREQUIRE), Sky_Render v1.20] [gf:53845 §15 River parts - 900 Gilda (5 entries), Bahamut4ever v1.75] [gf:53845 §15 Trees - 2100 Gilda (4 entries), Bahamut4ever v1.75]

### M167 Weapon Build-up / Synthesis
pointers: [wiki:darkcloud.fandom.com/Synthesis] [gf:36910 §Basic Gameplay (GDPLAY), Sky_Render v1.20]
row: M167
- A kill's ABS goes to the weapon in hand, and a weapon levels on ABS [wiki:darkcloud.fandom.com/Synthesis] [gf:36910 §Basic Gameplay (GDPLAY), Sky_Render v1.20]
- Each level pays Synthesis Points, three to six depending on the weapon, spent to add Synth Spheres to it [wiki:darkcloud.fandom.com/Synthesis] [gf:36910 §Basic Gameplay (GDPLAY), Sky_Render v1.20]
- Almost any item can be spectrumized into a Synth Sphere, and a weapon of +5 or higher can be turned into one too [wiki:darkcloud.fandom.com/Synthesis] [gf:36910 §Basic Gameplay (GDPLAY), Sky_Render v1.20]
- Once a weapon's attributes meet its requirements it can be Built Up into another weapon, and some have more than one to choose from [wiki:darkcloud.fandom.com/Synthesis] [gf:36910 §Basic Gameplay (GDPLAY), Sky_Render v1.20]
- Weapons carry WHP; a weapon that runs out breaks, but unlike in the first game it is not lost [wiki:darkcloud.fandom.com/Synthesis] [gf:36910 §Q. What's changed from Dark Cloud 1?, Sky_Render v1.20]

## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Hidden
- There are 396 medals, 303 of them reachable by the end of Chapter 7; each floor has a time goal, a clear goal and a Spheda or fishing goal [gf:36910 §Medal Collecting (GDMEDALS), Sky_Render v1.20]

### Upgrades
- A weapon that runs out of WHP stops doing damage until a Repair Powder of its type mends it [gf:36910 §Basic Gameplay (GDPLAY), Sky_Render v1.20]
- The Lure Rod can also be invented from the Fish, River and Lafrescia Stem photos [gf:58793 §Fishing Rods, Gothic89 v2.00]

### Shops & exchange
- Repair Powder comes in three kinds, one for wrenches, hammers, clubs and swords, one for guns and one for armbands [gf:36910 §Basic Gameplay (GDPLAY), Sky_Render v1.20]

## Unverified or contradicted

- **A hundred years on.** M166 said the rebuild restores the town's future "100 years on". The wiki's Ixion page puts the future a hundred years ahead; no guide read gives the number. Cut from the row [wiki:darkcloud.fandom.com/Ixion]
- **The residents' demands.** M166's examples were a botanist who wants trees nearby and a baker who wants bread delivered. Sky_Render and Bahamut4ever list the demands, and the baker, Aunt Polly, wants a cart outside her house; delivering Crunchy Bread to her is a recruitment step in Palm Brinks. The row now uses the listed demands [gf:36910 §A Stream and a Starlight Stair 10%, Sky_Render v1.20]
- **Ten stats and inherited growth.** M167 said Synth Spheres raise ten stats and that a built-up weapon inherits its growth. Sky_Render's sword table lists ten stat columns, but neither source says a new form inherits anything. Cut from the row [gf:36910 §Swords (GDSWORD), Sky_Render v1.20]
- **What a broken weapon costs.** Sky_Render says a weapon at zero WHP stops doing damage until repaired; the wiki says it loses half its current ABS. Each is one source, and the row says only that it breaks without being lost [gf:36910 §Basic Gameplay (GDPLAY), Sky_Render v1.20] [wiki:darkcloud.fandom.com/Synthesis]
- **Donny's middle levels.** Sky_Render gives level 3 a Bandit Coin, 4 an Explorer's Helmet, 5 Explorer's Shoes and 6 an Explorer's Outfit; Dark_Ryder gives an Explorer Cap, a Steal Coin, Explorer Boots and an Explorer Outfit. They agree on levels 2, 7 and 8 [gf:36910 §Inventing (GDINVENT), Sky_Render v1.20] [gf:21897 §Q: How do Score and Level work?, Dark_Ryder v4.2.5]
- **Three prizes per floor.** Turantula's list gives every floor a medal prize, a second-win prize and a repeating prize for every later win; PseudoPsychoSix says only that later prizes are lesser and was not sure they are fixed. The row says first win and later wins [gf:27622 §1.00 -- INTRODUCTION, Turantula v1.11] [gf:23048 §SECTION 4: SPHEDA PRIZE LIST, PseudoPsychoSix v1.0]
- **A treasure chest for every distortion.** g071 said each closed distortion spawns a chest. PseudoPsychoSix says so under a heading too long to cite; nothing else read does [gf:23048 §SECTION 4: SPHEDA PRIZE LIST, PseudoPsychoSix v1.0]
- **The contest's later prizes.** g072 listed a Flamingo club, stones, gems and a Missile Pod Arm for repeat Fishing Contest wins and Gold Bars for repeat Finny Frenzy wins. No source read gives them, and Sky_Render calls the contest prizes generally not worth the trouble. Cut from the row and its table [gf:36910 §Fishing Contest (GDCONTEST), Sky_Render v1.20]
- **Breeding and battling fish.** Gothic89 describes a breeding tank, where two fish of different kinds make a third, and a battle tank, where fish can die. No second source read covers either, so the row says only that fish are raised and raced [gf:58793 §Raising and Breeding Fish, Gothic89 v2.00]
- **Selling and cooking fish.** g072 said caught fish sell for Gilda or cook into healing food. No source read says so. Cut from the row [gf:58793 §Fishing Rods, Gothic89 v2.00]
