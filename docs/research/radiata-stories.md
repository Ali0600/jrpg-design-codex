### M183 Recruiting 170-odd NPCs (Friends List)
pointers: [wiki:radiata.fandom.com/Radiata Stories] [gf:42488 §Boss fight, DarkstarRipclaw] [gf:40883 §(reqs) Recruitment Requirements, wrp103 v6.0]
row: M183
- More than 170 named characters can be recruited: 176 by the wiki's count, 177 by DarkstarRipclaw's [wiki:radiata.fandom.com/Radiata Stories] [gf:42488 §Boss fight, DarkstarRipclaw]
- Some join after a conversation; others need a fetch quest, a won duel, a level reached, or a particular point in the story [wiki:radiata.fandom.com/Radiata Stories] [gf:40883 §(reqs) Recruitment Requirements, wrp103 v6.0]
- A recruit goes on the Friends List and can then join the party [wiki:radiata.fandom.com/Radiata Stories] [gf:40883 §(reqs) Recruitment Requirements, wrp103 v6.0]
- A few can only be recruited during a set period, and nobody can recruit everyone in one playthrough [wiki:radiata.fandom.com/Radiata Stories] [gf:40883 §(reqs) Recruitment Requirements, wrp103 v6.0] [gf:42488 §Boss fight, DarkstarRipclaw]
- A recruit's single skill can grow, but they learn no new ones and cannot be given other weapons or armour [wiki:radiata.fandom.com/Radiata Stories]

### M184 Simulated Day/Night NPC Schedules
pointers: [wiki:radiata.fandom.com/Radiata Stories] [gf:42488 §The Time System, DarkstarRipclaw]
row: M184
- Every character has a routine run by the day/night clock, and some things only happen at certain times [wiki:radiata.fandom.com/Radiata Stories] [gf:42488 §The Time System, DarkstarRipclaw]
- Jack can kick almost anything; furniture and objects sometimes drop items or dagols, the currency [wiki:radiata.fandom.com/Radiata Stories] [gf:42488 §Radiata Castle, DarkstarRipclaw]
- Kicking a person enough times starts a fight [wiki:radiata.fandom.com/Radiata Stories] [gf:40883 §(square) Large Man in Vancoor Square, wrp103 v6.0]

### M185 Human / Non-Human Path Fork
pointers: [wiki:radiata.fandom.com/Human Side] [wiki:radiata.fandom.com/Nonhuman Side] [gf:42488 §THE SPLIT THSL, DarkstarRipclaw]
row: M185
- Partway through, Jack chooses between meeting Larks at the castle (the Human side) and following Ridley (the Nonhuman side) [wiki:radiata.fandom.com/Human Side] [wiki:radiata.fandom.com/Nonhuman Side] [gf:42488 §THE SPLIT THSL, DarkstarRipclaw]
- The two sides offer different things to do and end differently, so the guides advise playing twice [wiki:radiata.fandom.com/Radiata Stories] [gf:42488 §THE SPLIT THSL, DarkstarRipclaw]
- The path decides which characters stay recruitable and which areas stay open; some already recruited are lost for that game [wiki:radiata.fandom.com/Radiata Stories] [gf:42488 §THE SPLIT THSL, DarkstarRipclaw]

## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Hidden
- One real minute is an hour of game time; day runs from 6am to 6pm, and Flora's painting can only be had from Synelia between 10am and 3pm [gf:42488 §The Time System, DarkstarRipclaw]

### Upgrades
- A new game started from a finished one keeps the Friends List in the menu, but everyone has to be recruited again to be used [wiki:radiata.fandom.com/Radiata Stories] [gf:40883 §(reqs) Recruitment Requirements, wrp103 v6.0]

### Shops & exchange
- A duel can be won against each character only once a day, and pays experience, money and sometimes items [wiki:radiata.fandom.com/Radiata Stories]

## Unverified or contradicted

- **176 or 177.** M183's name and text said exactly 176 recruitable characters. The wiki says 176, DarkstarRipclaw 177, and wrp103 gives no count. The row is renamed and says more than 170 [wiki:radiata.fandom.com/Radiata Stories] [gf:42488 §Boss fight, DarkstarRipclaw]
- **When the split comes.** The wiki puts it roughly halfway through the game; DarkstarRipclaw about two-thirds. The row says partway [wiki:radiata.fandom.com/Human Side] [gf:42488 §Boss fight, DarkstarRipclaw]
- **A fixed skill.** M183's notes called a recruit's skill fixed. The wiki says the skill can be strengthened, though no new one is learned. The notes now say so [wiki:radiata.fandom.com/Radiata Stories]
- **Thirty-odd missable recruits.** DarkstarRipclaw says thirty-some characters are missable; no second source read gives a figure [gf:42488 §Boss fight, DarkstarRipclaw]
