## Unverified or contradicted

- **A hollow sphere.** M203 called the planet a hollow sphere. The sources give it an outer face and an inner one, the Darkside's underworld, without the word; the row says two faces [gf:65645 §Using the Starstones..., KeyBlade999]
- **The ending's cost.** M203 said the Darkside's fate is bound to the Lightside's success at the end. Only the Nintendo Wiki describes the ending (the inner world collapses, and Ark sleeps for good); neither guide read does. Cut [wiki:nintendo.fandom.com/Terranigma]
- **Villages into cities.** M202 said villages grow into towns and towns into cities. The guides give five towns one or two levels each. Cut [gf:65645 §Dr. Beruga's Lab Tower - 1 Magirock, KeyBlade999]
- **No story gates.** M202 said growth is driven by errands rather than story gates; Loire's first growth waits on the king's death. Cut [gf:65645 §Loire, KeyBlade999]
- **Where Pierre works.** KeyBlade999's list puts Pierre's camera in Freedom; Telago meets him in Loire. The row does not say where [gf:65645 §Loire: Two Expansions, KeyBlade999] [gf:44583 §Loire (2), Telago v1.35]
- **The developers' intent.** M201's notes cite the developers choosing a creation theme so players imagine the consequences of their actions; no source read gives the studio's intent. The notes are left, as advice [gf:65645 §Evegreen, KeyBlade999]
