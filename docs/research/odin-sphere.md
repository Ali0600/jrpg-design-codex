## Mechanics candidates

### M177 Planting Seeds & Phozon Cultivation
pointers: [wiki:odinsphere.fandom.com/Phozons] [wiki:odinsphere.fandom.com/Odin Sphere leveling up] [gf:48610 §02. Seeds, HENTAIBEN] [gf:60596 §' ', vinheim]
row: M177
- Phozons are released when a creature dies, and seeds planted in the ground grow by absorbing them [wiki:odinsphere.fandom.com/Phozons] [gf:48610 §02. Seeds, HENTAIBEN]
- A grown plant bears fruit, or in the Baromett's case sheep, which are eaten to recover HP and gain the experience that raises Max HP [wiki:odinsphere.fandom.com/Odin Sphere leveling up] [gf:48610 §02. Seeds, HENTAIBEN] [gf:60596 §' ', vinheim]
- The Rosemile needs no Phozons and releases 12 instead, so planted beside another seed it grows that seed where no enemies are left [wiki:odinsphere.fandom.com/Rosemile Seed] [wiki:odinsphere.fandom.com/Odin Sphere leveling up] [gf:48610 §02. Seeds, HENTAIBEN]
- Seeds differ in the Phozons they need: 5 for a Mulberry, 8 for a Muggle, 12 for a Baromett or a Grape, 18 for a Napple, 30 for a Ruewort [wiki:odinsphere.fandom.com/Phozons]

### M178 Alchemy (found recipes, numbered Materials)
pointers: [wiki:odinsphere.fandom.com/Alchemy] [gf:48610 §V. Alchemy Recipes, HENTAIBEN] [gf:60596 §' ', vinheim]
row: M178
- Alchemy mixes a Material with a Mandragora, and there are five kinds of Mandragora [wiki:odinsphere.fandom.com/Alchemy] [gf:60596 §' ', vinheim]
- There are 26 recipes, lettered A to Z, and each must be found before it can be used [wiki:odinsphere.fandom.com/Alchemy] [gf:48610 §V. Alchemy Recipes, HENTAIBEN]
- A recipe names a Material number and a Mandragora: Material 2 and an Onionne make Napalm, Material 4 and an Onionne make Drain [wiki:odinsphere.fandom.com/Alchemy] [gf:60596 §' ', vinheim]
- Mixing anything into a Material that does not complete a recipe raises its number — a seed by 1, a fruit by 5, a bone by 3, a potion doubles it — which is how a Material is built up to the number a recipe wants [gf:48610 §V. Alchemy Recipes, HENTAIBEN] [gf:60596 §' ', vinheim]

### M179 Pooka Restaurant Cooking (Recipe Feasts)
pointers: [wiki:odinsphere.fandom.com/Cooking] [wiki:odinsphere.fandom.com/Chicken Au Gratin] [gf:48610 §28. Chicken Au Gratin, HENTAIBEN] [gf:60596 §' ', vinheim]
row: M179
- The Pooka village has two restaurants, and their dishes give experience and raise Max HP as well as healing, far beyond ordinary food [wiki:odinsphere.fandom.com/Cooking] [gf:60596 §' ', vinheim]
- A dish needs its recipe, its ingredients and a coin: Chicken Au Gratin takes Chicken, Cheese and an Ariel Coin and gives 1,500 EXP and +8 Max HP [wiki:odinsphere.fandom.com/Chicken Au Gratin] [gf:48610 §28. Chicken Au Gratin, HENTAIBEN]
- Eating a dish enough times reveals a secret recipe: five Chicken Au Gratins unlock Smothered Chicken [wiki:odinsphere.fandom.com/Chicken Au Gratin] [gf:48610 §29. Smothered Chicken, HENTAIBEN]

## Unverified or contradicted

- **Material grade and potion strength.** M178 said a Material's grade scales the potion's strength and that finished potions level up by re-mixing. HENTAIBEN says a Material's last digit decides the potion and its first how many Phozons the mix releases; the wiki says Material levels raising potion power is the remake's system. Cut from the row [gf:48610 §V. Alchemy Recipes, HENTAIBEN] [wiki:odinsphere.fandom.com/Alchemy]
- **Recipes from enemies.** The wiki says new Alchemy recipes are gained from enemies; the guides read say only that each must be found. The row says found [wiki:odinsphere.fandom.com/Alchemy]
- **Overripe fruit.** HENTAIBEN says a fruit left too long overripens and does less; no second source read says so [gf:48610 §02. Seeds, HENTAIBEN]
- **Recipes from stage-clear chests.** M179 said restaurant recipes come mostly from stage-clear chests. HENTAIBEN gives each recipe's chapter; no source read says where most come from. Cut from the row [gf:48610 §28. Chicken Au Gratin, HENTAIBEN]
- **Food as the main way to level.** M177 called eating grown food the primary way to level up. The sources say food raises the HP level; the Psypher levels separately on Phozons. The row says what food raises [wiki:odinsphere.fandom.com/Phozons]
