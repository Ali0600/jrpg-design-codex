## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Hidden
- Some properties can only be bought once certain characters' friendship is maxed out [wiki:yakuza.fandom.com/Real Estate Royale]

### Upgrades
- Each style's tree has six rings; the outer two open only through the character's business [gf:74451 §Upgrading, CyricZ v1.7]
- An ability taught by a Master is free on the tree once his training is passed [gf:74451 §Upgrading, CyricZ v1.7]

### Shops & exchange
- Properties cost from about fifteen million yen, rising in later districts [gf:74451 §Much More Money -, CyricZ v1.7]

## Unverified or contradicted

- **When it opens.** The wiki unlocks Real Estate Royale after reaching Chapter 6; CyricZ begins it in Chapter 5. The row names no chapter [wiki:yakuza.fandom.com/Real Estate Royale] [gf:74451 §Real Estate Royale, CyricZ v1.7]
- **Majima's style.** CyricZ unlocks Majima's Mad Dog of Shimano style by finishing Cabaret Club Czar; no second source read says so. Cut from g083 [gf:74451 §Goro Majima: Legend Style, CyricZ v1.7]
- **Friendships.** The wiki says Real Estate Royale lets Kiryu max out friendships otherwise locked; CyricZ's pages read do not. Cut from g082's table [wiki:yakuza.fandom.com/Real Estate Royale]
- **Live matching.** CyricZ describes shifts spent matching hostesses to clients; no second source read describes the shifts. The row says the club is run, not how [gf:74451 §Cabaret Club Czar, CyricZ v1.7]
- **Hostesses who switch clubs.** The wiki says each area's beaten club sends its number one hostess to Majima; CyricZ's pages read do not cover the rival battles [wiki:yakuza.fandom.com/Club Sunshine]
- **The share needed.** CyricZ gives a 90% share per district; the wiki gives events at 30% and 60%. The table says only that the share must rise [gf:74451 §Why All This Money? -, CyricZ v1.7] [wiki:yakuza.fandom.com/Real Estate Royale]
