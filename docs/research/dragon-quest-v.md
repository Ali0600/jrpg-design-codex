## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Hidden
- An item combination kept in the hero's inventory makes every recruitable monster want to join, per Khronlk's secrets section [gf:46428 §7. Secrets of the Game, Khronlk vFinal]

### Upgrades
- The wagon is bought in Oraclebury, and a man there, Mr. Monster, explains recruiting [gf:59751 §08. MONSTER RECRUITMENT, threetimes v1.0]

### Shops & exchange
- Marrying the rich man's daughter brings some extra money, the PS2 guide says [gf:59751 §Who To Choose?, threetimes v1.0]

## Unverified or contradicted

- **A five-place wagon.** The wiki gives the Super Famicom wagon five places; Khronlk gives no number, and the PS2 guide's wagon holds four. The row gives no number [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Dragon Quest V] [gf:59751 §08. MONSTER RECRUITMENT, threetimes v1.0]
- **Orders.** The wiki says recruits take orders like anyone else; the PS2 guide says a monster ignores commands until its Wisdom reaches 20. The row leaves orders out [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Dragon Quest V] [gf:59751 §08. MONSTER RECRUITMENT, threetimes v1.0]
- **Very low odds.** M214 said many species join very rarely; Khronlk rates each from very easy to very hard without odds. The row says each has its own chance [gf:46428 §9. Recruitable Monster List, Khronlk vFinal]
- **A family reshaped.** M215 said the choice reshapes the family you finish with; no source read says what differs beyond the bride. Cut [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Dragon Quest V]
