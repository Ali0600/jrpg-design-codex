## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Shops & exchange

## Unverified or contradicted

- **A value that decays.** M209 said a discovery's price depends on how well it was hidden and falls the longer it has been findable; no source read says so. Cut, and the notes' advice on decay with it [wiki:skiesofarcadia.fandom.com/Discoveries] [gf:9523 §V. How to find new discoveries ?, Kenshin_Leo v1.0]
- **Eighty-nine.** M209 counted 89 Discoveries, the GameCube's number; the Dreamcast has 64 [gf:9523 §Introduction, Kenshin_Leo v1.0]
- **Domingo the rival.** The wiki notes that once recruited Domingo can no longer make discoveries himself, which implies he makes them before; no guide read says how [wiki:skiesofarcadia.fandom.com/Crew Members]
- **Shopkeepers who notice.** The wiki says shopkeepers start using Vyse's title at Determined and all do at Respected; no guide read says so. Cut from M211 [wiki:skiesofarcadia.fandom.com/Swashbuckler Rating]
- **The whole island's mood.** M210 said funding the crew visibly improves the island's mood and appearance; the sources give upgraded services and stock, and a redesign by two crew members. The row says services [wiki:skiesofarcadia.fandom.com/Crescent Isle]
