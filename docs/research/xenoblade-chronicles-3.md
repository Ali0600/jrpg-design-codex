### M051 Exploration EXP (discovering places levels you)
pointers: [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Experience Points (XC3)] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/383914]
row: M051
- Discovering landmarks pays EXP, and so do Collectopaedia Cards and discussing topics at a Rest Spot [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Experience Points (XC3)]; Game8 names discovering new locations and landmarks, Rest Spot topics and skirmishes as sources of Bonus EXP, and adds that special locations pay SP as well [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/383914]
- Only EXP from defeating enemies is added to a character at once; every other source is converted into Bonus EXP [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Experience Points (XC3)]
- Bonus EXP is spent to level characters up at a Rest Spot [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Rest Spot (XC3)] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/383914]; Game8 adds the colony barracks [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/383914]
- After the ending a Rest Spot can also lower a level, returning the excess as Bonus EXP [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Experience Points (XC3)]

### M052 Hero Quests (people are unlockables)
pointers: [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Hero] [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Hero Quest] [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Class (XC3)] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/380172] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/380118]
row: M052
- Heroes are guest characters; completing a Hero's Hero Quest makes them usable in the party and unlocks their class [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Hero Quest] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/380172]
- A recruited Hero fights as a seventh party member, not in place of one of the six [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Hero] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/380172]
- Not every Hero comes by a Hero Quest: Ghondor joins through the main story [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Hero] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/380172], and Melia and Nia join in their castles after the story ends [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/380172]
- Each playable character's aptitude for a class, graded S to D, sets how fast they unlock it and rank it up [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Class (XC3)] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/380118]; the character with S aptitude inherits a Hero's class the moment the Hero is recruited [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Class (XC3)]

### M053 Class inheritance + Ascension Quests
pointers: [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Class Rank (XC3)] [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Class (XC3)] [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Hero Quest] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/380118] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/385522]
row: M053
- Ranking a class up earns new Arts and Skills, some of them as Master Arts and Master Skills that the character keeps using in other classes [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Class (XC3)] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/380118]
- Master Arts go only on a class of the opposite nation, and feed Fusion Arts [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Class (XC3)]
- Ranks 1 and 10 unlock a Master Art, ranks 5 and 15 a Master Skill, and ranks 5, 10, 15 and 20 upgrade the class's Arts and Skills [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Class Rank (XC3)]
- The first cap is rank 10. A Hero's Ascension Quest opens once a party member reaches rank 10 in that Hero's class, and completing it raises the cap to 20 [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Hero Quest] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/385522]
- Ascension Quests unlock no new character or class [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/385522]; the six base classes are raised by Side Story quests instead [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Class Rank (XC3)]

### M054 Traversal skills taught by Heroes
pointers: [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Traversal Skill] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/382844]
row: M054
- There are four traversal skills, wall climbing, rope sliding, scree walking and hazard neutralization, and some places can only be reached with them [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Traversal Skill] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/382844]
- Wall climbing comes from Valdi's Hero Quest "The Kind Right Hand", scree walking from Teach's "Going Beyond Power" and hazard neutralization from Segiri's "Inhumanity" [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/382844]; the wiki says the skills are acquired through Hero Quests [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Traversal Skill]
- Scree walking crosses quicksand, and hazard neutralization cancels a hazardous surface's health drain [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/382844]
- The walls a climber can scale are marked on the map [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Traversal Skill] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/382844]

### M055 Collectopaedia Cards
pointers: [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Collectopaedia (XC3)] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/383673]
row: M055
- NPCs hand out Collectopaedia Cards, each listing items to gather, up to ten kinds on one card [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Collectopaedia (XC3)] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/383673]
- A full card is handed in from the Quests menu, anywhere on the map, without going back to the NPC [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Collectopaedia (XC3)] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/383673]
- The first completion pays affinity with the NPC's colony and an item, often an accessory (Colony 9's Pollux: +30 affinity and a Friendship Ring) [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/383673] [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Collectopaedia (XC3)]
- A card can be filled again for less: usually a fifth of the affinity, with EXP in place of the item (Pollux's repeat: 40 EXP and +6 affinity) [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Collectopaedia (XC3)] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/383673]
- Silver Nopon Coins can complete a card without the items, except a Nopon Caravan card [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Collectopaedia (XC3)]

### M056 Colony liberation + affinity webs
pointers: [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Affinity (XC3)] [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Colony] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/384734] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/380552]
row: M056
- A colony is freed from its Flame Clock, and its people can then be helped [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Colony] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/380552]
- Affinity with a colony rises by completing its quests, completing its people's Collectopaedia Cards and sending off the husks of its fallen soldiers [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Affinity (XC3)] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/380552]
- A colony's affinity goes up to five levels, and each level pays one travel perk [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Affinity (XC3)] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/380552]; Game8 lists Colony Omega and Colony 5 as paying none [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/384734]
- The perks are Speed Jogger (running speed), Item Retriever (item collection range), Silent Steps (monster detection range), Shiny-Hunter (rare monsters), Slow Digestion (meal effects), Swift Swimmer (swimming speed) and Caravan Call (Nopon Caravans visit the colonies) [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Affinity (XC3)] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/384734]

### M057 Unique Monsters (named world bosses)
pointers: [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Unique Monster (XC3)] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/383707]
row: M057
- Unique Monsters have their own names, only one of each exists in the world, and they are much stronger than ordinary enemies [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Unique Monster (XC3)] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/383707]
- Game8 lists each one at its place on the region maps, from Sycophantic Lilith (level 5, Yzana Plains) to Perilwing Ryuho (level 100) [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/383707]; the wiki counts 141 in the base game [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Unique Monster (XC3)]
- Defeating one raises a Named Grave on the spot, which records the fastest time and the difficulty and revives the monster for a rematch when used [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Unique Monster (XC3)] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/383707]
- The party can fast travel to any Named Grave [wiki:xenoblade.fandom.com/Unique Monster (XC3)] [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/383707]
- Some appear only at a certain time of day or in certain weather, and their rematches need the same [web:game8.co/games/Xenoblade-Chronicles-3/archives/383707]

- **Containers and paths that tease locked routes.** M054 said so, and `docs/verbs.md` quoted it for *Visible but unreachable*.
