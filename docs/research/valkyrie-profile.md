### M128 Einherjar (recruit the dead, train them, give them away)
pointers: [wiki:valkyrieprofile.fandom.com/Einherjar] [wiki:valkyrieprofile.fandom.com/Valkyrie Profile] [gf:12648 §5) Transferring characters to Asgard, Captain_K v4.0] [gf:6628 §2.2)Status Screen, SBruck v1.6]
row: M128
- Einherjar are souls of the dead recruited to Odin's army, found through Spiritual Concentration [wiki:valkyrieprofile.fandom.com/Einherjar] [wiki:valkyrieprofile.fandom.com/Valkyrie Profile] [gf:6628 §4.4)Chapter 3, SBruck v1.6]
- Trained warriors are transferred to Valhalla; the requirement starts with a positive Hero value, and each chapter asks for more [gf:12648 §5) Transferring characters to Asgard, Captain_K v4.0] [gf:6628 §2.2)Status Screen, SBruck v1.6]
- Transfers pay: extra Materialize Points in the chapter's Sacred Phase, and items from Odin set by the Hero Value of everyone sent [gf:12648 §5) Transferring characters to Asgard, Captain_K v4.0] [gf:6628 §Sacred Phase 1-2, SBruck v1.6]
- Sending no one lowers Lenneth's evaluation, and at zero the game ends badly [wiki:valkyrieprofile.fandom.com/Valkyrie Profile] [gf:6628 §2.4)Time, SBruck v1.6]

### M129 Period economy (chapters of limited actions)
pointers: [gf:6628 §2.4)Time, SBruck v1.6] [gf:12648 §Q: Where's a good place to, Captain_K v4.0] [wiki:valkyrieprofile.fandom.com/Valkyrie Profile]
row: M129
- The game runs in chapters of periods; each event costs 1 or 2 periods, and when they run out the next chapter begins [gf:6628 §2.4)Time, SBruck v1.6] [gf:12648 §Q: Where's a good place to, Captain_K v4.0]
- Every major event in a chapter can be done in its periods [gf:6628 §2.4)Time, SBruck v1.6]
- A chapter ends in a Sacred Phase, which pays Materialize Points [gf:12648 §5) Transferring characters to Asgard, Captain_K v4.0] [gf:6628 §Sacred Phase 1-2, SBruck v1.6]
- Spiritual Concentration shows where to go next [gf:6628 §4.4)Chapter 3, SBruck v1.6] [gf:12648 §18) Endings, Captain_K v4.0]

### M130 Photon-crystal platforming dungeons
pointers: [gf:12648 §4) Crystal tricks, Captain_K v4.0] [gf:6628 §Mount Altoria Ruins, SBruck v1.6]
row: M130
- Dungeons are 2D platforming spaces [gf:12648 §1) Introduction, Captain_K v4.0] [gf:6628 §Mount Altoria Ruins, SBruck v1.6]
- A button fires a blast that forms a crystal on a solid surface, and Lenneth can stand on it to reach higher places [gf:12648 §4) Crystal tricks, Captain_K v4.0] [gf:6628 §Mount Altoria Ruins, SBruck v1.6]
- Crystals can be grown, cracked and broken into blocks that can be carried, thrown or floated on water as stepping stones [gf:12648 §4) Crystal tricks, Captain_K v4.0]

## Unverified or contradicted

- **Whether transferred warriors return.** Captain_K says most come back at the end of the game; SBruck says a transferred character never comes back. The row says only that they leave the party [gf:12648 §5) Transferring characters to Asgard, Captain_K v4.0] [gf:6628 §2.2)Status Screen, SBruck v1.6]
- **Freezing enemies into platforms and weights.** M130 said the photon shot freezes enemies into crystals that serve as platforms, switch weights and stepping stones. SBruck says the crystals can briefly freeze enemies; neither guide says a frozen enemy becomes a platform or a weight, and Captain_K's crystal section describes crystals on surfaces only. The row, its loop, its notes and its `docs/verbs.md` quote are rewritten [gf:6628 §Mount Altoria Ruins, SBruck v1.6] [gf:12648 §4) Crystal tricks, Captain_K v4.0]
- **A divine evaluation at each chapter's end.** M129 said so. Both guides describe the Sacred Phase paying Materialize Points and items; the evaluation is a running value, so the row names the Sacred Phase instead [gf:12648 §5) Transferring characters to Asgard, Captain_K v4.0] [gf:6628 §Sacred Phase 1-2, SBruck v1.6]
