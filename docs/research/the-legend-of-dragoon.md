### M133 Additions
pointers: [wiki:legendofdragoon.fandom.com/Additions] [gf:6526 §HINTS ON ADDITIONAL, Happy_Matt v0.7] [gf:49555 §02) Forest (WK02), Shotgunnova vMeru]
row: M133
- The Attack command opens a timed combo: an outer square shrinks onto a fixed central one and the button is pressed at the overlap to chain the next hit [wiki:legendofdragoon.fandom.com/Additions] [gf:6526 §HINTS ON ADDITIONAL, Happy_Matt v0.7]
- The number of timed inputs is the Addition's hit count minus one; the chosen Addition is set outside battle and cannot be changed during one [wiki:legendofdragoon.fandom.com/Additions] [gf:6526 §HINTS ON ADDITIONAL, Happy_Matt v0.7]
- An enemy can counter mid-combo: the square changes colour and the other button is wanted; missing it ends the Addition and damages the character [wiki:legendofdragoon.fandom.com/Additions] [gf:6526 §HINTS ON ADDITIONAL, Happy_Matt v0.7]
- Each completed Addition adds one to its own use-counter, and every 20 raise its level, which raises its SP and its damage bonus [wiki:legendofdragoon.fandom.com/Additions] [gf:6526 §HINTS ON ADDITIONAL, Happy_Matt v0.7] [gf:49555 §02) Forest (WK02), Shotgunnova vMeru]
- Level 5 is the maximum, reached at 80 completions; the counter runs to 99 and nothing changes past 80 [wiki:legendofdragoon.fandom.com/Additions] [gf:6526 §HINTS ON ADDITIONAL, Happy_Matt v0.7]
- New Additions unlock at set character levels, except each character's last, which unlocks by mastering all the others [wiki:legendofdragoon.fandom.com/Additions]
- Two accessories auto-complete Additions: the Wargod Calling at half damage, half SP and no counter tick, the Ultimate Wargod at full value and it does tick [wiki:legendofdragoon.fandom.com/Additions] [gf:49555 §25) Phantom Ship (WK25), Shotgunnova vMeru]

### M134 Dragoon Transformation & Dragoon Level
pointers: [wiki:legendofdragoon.fandom.com/Dragoon] [gf:6526 §SPIRIT POINT AND DRAGOON LEVEL, Happy_Matt v0.7] [gf:49555 §BATTLE, Shotgunnova vMeru]
row: M134
- SP is earned mainly by completing Additions, and how much depends on the Addition and its level [wiki:legendofdragoon.fandom.com/Dragoon] [gf:6526 §SPIRIT POINT AND DRAGOON LEVEL, Happy_Matt v0.7]
- 100 SP is one bar and buys one turn as a Dragoon; the character cannot return to human form until the bars run out or the battle ends [wiki:legendofdragoon.fandom.com/Dragoon] [gf:6526 §SPIRIT POINT AND DRAGOON LEVEL, Happy_Matt v0.7]
- A Dragoon gets raised stats and elemental Dragoon magic, and is immune to status ailments — instant death excepted [wiki:legendofdragoon.fandom.com/Dragoon] [gf:6526 §SPIRIT POINT AND DRAGOON LEVEL, Happy_Matt v0.7]
- Every SP earned also becomes hidden experience for a separate Dragoon Level, which runs 1 to 5 and keeps accruing while the gauge is full [wiki:legendofdragoon.fandom.com/Dragoon] [gf:6526 §SPIRIT POINT AND DRAGOON LEVEL, Happy_Matt v0.7]
- Each Dragoon Level adds a bar (5 at level 5), raises the Dragoon's stats and raises maximum MP [wiki:legendofdragoon.fandom.com/Dragoon] [gf:6526 §SPIRIT POINT AND DRAGOON LEVEL, Happy_Matt v0.7] [gf:49555 §BATTLE, Shotgunnova vMeru]
- New Dragoon magic arrives at Dragoon Levels 2, 3 and 5, on top of the level-1 spell [wiki:legendofdragoon.fandom.com/Dragoon] [gf:6526 §LIST OF DRAGOON MAGIC, Happy_Matt v0.7]
- The wiki says battle experience moves only the character level, never the Addition or Dragoon level; Happy_Matt gives SP as the one way to raise a Dragoon Level [wiki:legendofdragoon.fandom.com/Dragoon] [gf:6526 §SPIRIT POINT AND DRAGOON LEVEL, Happy_Matt v0.7]

## Unverified or contradicted

- **The arena's four games.** The wiki names an obstacle course, a spot-the-difference room ("What's Wrong With The Scene?"), a shell game ("Who is the Man") and a ball-dodging bout ("Monster Conquest"). No second source describes them: 49555 sends the reader to a minigame section it does not carry, and JTan's FAQ covers only the PocketStation game. g055 now says the arena holds skill games without counting or naming them [wiki:legendofdragoon.fandom.com/Lohan] [gf:49555 §13) Commercial Town of Lohan (II) (WK13), Shotgunnova vMeru]
- **Which colour the counter square turns.** The wiki says the squares turn red and the circle button is wanted; Happy_Matt says the red square turns blue and the Cancel button is wanted. Both agree the colour changes and the other button is pressed, which is what the row says [wiki:legendofdragoon.fandom.com/Additions] [gf:6526 §HINTS ON ADDITIONAL, Happy_Matt v0.7]
- **Flight in Dragoon form.** The wiki says a Dragoon gains the ability to fly. Happy_Matt's Dragoon section lists raised stats, magic and status immunity and never mentions flight, and 49555's battle section does not either, so M134 no longer claims it [wiki:legendofdragoon.fandom.com/Dragoon] [gf:6526 §SPIRIT POINT AND DRAGOON LEVEL, Happy_Matt v0.7]
- **The Stardust prize percentages.** The wiki gives the Physical Ring +50% maximum HP and the Amulet double maximum MP. EmPleh's notes support the effects but give no figures, and no second source carries either number, so the row names the effects without them. The Wargod Sash's 50% is kept, since 49555 states it [wiki:legendofdragoon.fandom.com/Stardust] [gf:30399 §Accessory Notes, EmPleh v1.35] [gf:49555 §23) Twin Castle at Fletz (III) (WK23), Shotgunnova vMeru]
- **Whether the sash is "Wargod Sash" or "Wargod's Sash".** The wiki and EmPleh write Wargod Sash; Grand_Lethal and Shotgunnova write Wargod's Sash. The row follows the wiki [wiki:legendofdragoon.fandom.com/Stardust] [gf:30399 §Accessory Notes, EmPleh v1.35] [gf:11802 §10 Stardust: Physical Ring (4 entries), Grand_Lethal]
- **The Talisman shielding "a Dragoon".** g056 said the Talisman shields a Dragoon from instant death. It is an ordinary accessory that defends against instant death for whoever wears it, and a Dragoon is already immune to everything but instant death. The row now says what it does [gf:49555 §25) Phantom Ship (WK25), Shotgunnova vMeru] [wiki:legendofdragoon.fandom.com/Dragoon]
- **Spending SP versus banking it.** M134's loop offered a choice between transforming now and banking SP for the Dragoon Level. There is no choice: every SP earned counts toward the level whether or not the gauge is spent, and it keeps counting when the gauge is already full [gf:6526 §SPIRIT POINT AND DRAGOON LEVEL, Happy_Matt v0.7] [wiki:legendofdragoon.fandom.com/Dragoon]
