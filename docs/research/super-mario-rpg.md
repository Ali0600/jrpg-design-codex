### M198 Action Commands (Timed Hits)
pointers: [wiki:www.mariowiki.com/Timed Hit] [gf:30935 §T Values, newmansage v1.03] [gf:30935 §ON TIMED HITS (WPNTH), newmansage v1.03] [gf:30935 §DEFENSE BASICS (DEFBS), newmansage v1.03]
row: M198
- A weapon attack timed well does half again as much damage, and timed perfectly double [wiki:www.mariowiki.com/Timed Hit] [gf:30935 §T Values, newmansage v1.03]
- A physical blow against the party, timed well, does half damage, and timed perfectly none; special attacks and some physical ones cannot be blocked [wiki:www.mariowiki.com/Timed Hit] [gf:30935 §DEFENSE BASICS (DEFBS), newmansage v1.03]
- The timing multiplies the damage once it is calculated and raises no stat [wiki:www.mariowiki.com/Timed Hit] [gf:30935 §DEFENSE BASICS (DEFBS), newmansage v1.03] [gf:30935 §ON TIMED HITS (WPNTH), newmansage v1.03]
- Special attacks use other inputs too, among them a button held until the attack charges [wiki:www.mariowiki.com/Timed Hit] [gf:30935 §3, newmansage v1.03]

### M199 Hidden Treasure Boxes (room-scale curiosity)
pointers: [wiki:www.mariowiki.com/Hidden Treasure] [wiki:www.mariowiki.com/Signal Ring] [gf:5656 §MUSHROOM KINGDOM, NickWhiz1] [gf:5656 §SIGNAL RING, NickWhiz1] [gf:5656 §BEAN VALLEY, NickWhiz1]
row: M199
- Invisible treasure boxes appear when Mario jumps in the right spot [wiki:www.mariowiki.com/Hidden Treasure] [gf:5656 §MUSHROOM KINGDOM, NickWhiz1]
- There are more than thirty; the walkthrough numbers them past 31 and the wiki counts 39 [gf:5656 §BEAN VALLEY, NickWhiz1] [wiki:www.mariowiki.com/Hidden Treasure]
- Many are indoors, in shops and houses: the Mushroom Kingdom's item shop basement, Rose Town's shop and a house on its hill [wiki:www.mariowiki.com/Hidden Treasure] [gf:5656 §MUSHROOM KINGDOM, NickWhiz1] [gf:5656 §Trueform Pin| 60 coins, NickWhiz1]
- The Signal Ring, an accessory, sounds a tone when an undiscovered one is in the room [gf:5656 §SIGNAL RING, NickWhiz1] [wiki:www.mariowiki.com/Signal Ring]

### M200 Super Jump chain (mastery as a reward track)
pointers: [wiki:www.mariowiki.com/Super Jump (Super Mario RPG)] [wiki:www.mariowiki.com/Attack Scarf] [wiki:www.mariowiki.com/Super Suit] [gf:5661 §2.1 What are Super Jumps?, RRussell v1.0] [gf:5661 §THE KEY TO BETTER SUPER JUMPING, RRussell v1.0] [gf:5661 §3.2 Shoot for 100 Super Jumps, RRussell v1.0]
row: M200
- Super Jump is Mario's second special attack; it stomps one enemy again for every timed press, up to 100 in a row [gf:5661 §2.1 What are Super Jumps?, RRussell v1.0] [wiki:www.mariowiki.com/Super Jump (Super Mario RPG)] [gf:5661 §3.2 Shoot for 100 Super Jumps, RRussell v1.0]
- A Chow, a dog, in Monstro Town gives the Attack Scarf for 30 in a row and the Super Suit for 100 [wiki:www.mariowiki.com/Super Jump (Super Mario RPG)] [gf:5661 §THE KEY TO BETTER SUPER JUMPING, RRussell v1.0] [gf:5661 §3.2 Shoot for 100 Super Jumps, RRussell v1.0]
- The Attack Scarf, which only Mario wears, adds 30 to attack, defence and speed; the Super Suit, which anyone wears, adds 50 to attack and defence and 30 to speed [wiki:www.mariowiki.com/Attack Scarf] [wiki:www.mariowiki.com/Super Suit] [gf:5661 §SECTION 4: Your Rewards, RRussell v1.0]

## Unverified or contradicted

- **Four named kinds.** M198 named Timed Hits, Continuous Hits, Star Hits and Rotational Hits; only the wiki names all four. The row says special attacks use other inputs, such as a held button [wiki:www.mariowiki.com/Timed Hit]
- **Timing on every defence.** M198 said every defence has a window; special attacks and some physical blows cannot be blocked. The row says most physical blows [gf:30935 §DEFENSE BASICS (DEFBS), newmansage v1.03]
- **Nothing signposts them.** M199 said nothing marks the boxes; the Signal Ring sounds for them, and the wiki has an elderly Mushroom person in the item shop basement teach Mario the first. Corrected [gf:5656 §SIGNAL RING, NickWhiz1] [wiki:www.mariowiki.com/Hidden Treasure]
- **Level 6.** The wiki has Mario learn Super Jump at level 6; RRussell says only that it is his second special after the Fire Orb. The row says second special [wiki:www.mariowiki.com/Super Jump (Super Mario RPG)]
- **Who teaches the boxes.** The wiki's item-shop teacher is an elderly woman; _PContaminator_'s list calls the same figure a man. Neither is in the row [wiki:www.mariowiki.com/Hidden Treasure]
