## Mechanics candidates

The four existing rows, sourced on 2026-09-16. No new rows: M028–M031 are edited in place.

### M028 Paintings → vista treasure
pointers: [wiki:eldenring.fandom.com/Paintings] [web:game8.co/games/Elden-Ring/archives/369333]
row: M028
- Paintings are info items scattered across the Lands Between, each a scenic view made by the Artist [wiki:eldenring.fandom.com/Paintings] [web:game8.co/games/Elden-Ring/archives/369333]
- Reaching the place a painting depicts — the point of perspective it was painted from — makes the Artist's apparition appear, and a unique item can be taken from the spot [wiki:eldenring.fandom.com/Paintings] [web:game8.co/games/Elden-Ring/archives/369333]
- Collected paintings can be looked at again in the Info section [web:game8.co/games/Elden-Ring/archives/369333]

### M029 Unmarked world density (every landmark pays off)
pointers: [wiki:eldenring.fandom.com/Landmarks] [wiki:eldenring.fandom.com/Catacombs] [web:game8.co/games/Elden-Ring/archives/459776] [web:game8.co/games/Elden-Ring/archives/370639]
row: M029
- A named landmark appears on the map only once the location has been discovered [wiki:eldenring.fandom.com/Landmarks]
- Catacombs are maze-like underground dungeons with traps, puzzles and at least one boss, entered by a door in the overworld and a descending lift [wiki:eldenring.fandom.com/Catacombs]
- Game8's dungeon list gives each dungeon its bosses, caves and gaols among them as well as catacombs [web:game8.co/games/Elden-Ring/archives/459776]
- Caelid Catacombs, one such minor dungeon, ends at the Cemetery Shade, which pays the Kindred of Rot Ashes [web:game8.co/games/Elden-Ring/archives/370639]

### M030 Golden Seeds & Sacred Tears
pointers: [wiki:eldenring.fandom.com/Golden Seed] [wiki:eldenring.fandom.com/Sacred Tear] [wiki:eldenring.fandom.com/Flask of Crimson Tears] [web:game8.co/games/Elden-Ring/archives/367585]
row: M030
- A Golden Seed, found at the base of an illusory tree, increases a sacred flask's number of uses; a Sacred Tear, a blessing worshipped in the churches, increases its potency. Both are used after resting at a site of grace [wiki:eldenring.fandom.com/Golden Seed] [wiki:eldenring.fandom.com/Sacred Tear]
- Seeds raise the flasks' total charges, which are then split between the crimson and cerulean flasks at any site of grace; a Golden Seed can also be chosen as a starting keepsake [web:game8.co/games/Elden-Ring/archives/367585]
- The Flask of Crimson Tears restores 250 HP at +0 and 810 at +12 [wiki:eldenring.fandom.com/Flask of Crimson Tears]
- Sacred Tears are kept in churches such as the Third Church of Marika and Callu Baptismal Church [wiki:eldenring.fandom.com/Sacred Tear]

### M031 Illusory walls & secret-behind-secret
pointers: [web:game8.co/games/Elden-Ring/archives/369065] [wiki:eldenring.fandom.com/Road's End Catacombs] [wiki:eldenring.fandom.com/Messages]
row: M031
- Illusory walls are fake walls hiding other rooms or paths, and the secret areas can hold valuable items or weapons; a hit with a weapon or a roll through one dispels it [web:game8.co/games/Elden-Ring/archives/369065]
- Some need many hits: the first multi-hit wall the community found took about 50, and it sits in the hallway behind another illusory wall [web:game8.co/games/Elden-Ring/archives/369065]
- Illusory walls obscure the route through Road's End Catacombs, and appear in the Subterranean Shunning-Grounds too [wiki:eldenring.fandom.com/Road's End Catacombs] [wiki:eldenring.fandom.com/Subterranean Shunning-Grounds]
- Messages let a player leave cryptic hints, helpful or deceptive, at a spot for players in other worlds; a rated message restores some of its writer's HP [wiki:eldenring.fandom.com/Messages]
