### M180 Item Creation / Inventions
pointers: [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Item Creation] [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Workshop] [gf:24563 §STAR OCEAN: TILL THE END OF TIME, skuldnoshinpu v1.5]
row: M180
- There are eight trades — Cooking, Crafting, Smithery, Engineering, Alchemy, Compounding, Writing and Synthesis [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Item Creation] [gf:24563 §STAR OCEAN: TILL THE END OF TIME, skuldnoshinpu v1.5]
- Item Creation happens only at workshops scattered across the world; each is equipped for some trades, and more trades and more production lines are bought with Fol [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Workshop] [gf:24563 §STAR OCEAN: TILL THE END OF TIME, skuldnoshinpu v1.5]
- A line is worked by party members or hired Inventors, new ones announced as the game goes on [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Inventor] [gf:24563 §STAR OCEAN: TILL THE END OF TIME, skuldnoshinpu v1.5]
- A line either invents an item from scratch or refines one you own, and an attempt can fail [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Item Creation] [gf:24563 §STAR OCEAN: TILL THE END OF TIME, skuldnoshinpu v1.5]
- Any item can be patented, after which it goes on sale in shops [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Item Creation] [gf:24563 §STAR OCEAN: TILL THE END OF TIME, skuldnoshinpu v1.5]

### M181 Battle Trophies (Battle Collection)
pointers: [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Battle Trophy] [gf:23865 §TABLE OF CONTENTS (5 entries), skuldnoshinpu v1.6]
row: M181
- The Battle Collection is 300 conditions met in battle, each logged with the date and time [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Battle Trophy] [gf:23865 §TABLE OF CONTENTS (5 entries), skuldnoshinpu v1.6]
- Some can only be earned on the Universe and 4D modes, against the superbosses [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Battle Trophy] [gf:23865 §2. CONDITION LISTING, skuldnoshinpu v1.6]
- Completing shares of the collection unlocks the Universe and 4D difficulty modes, the Music Test and Full Active mode [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Battle Trophy] [gf:23865 §TABLE OF CONTENTS (5 entries), skuldnoshinpu v1.6]

### M182 Synthesis / Refinement (Factors)
pointers: [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Synthesis] [gf:24563 §STAR OCEAN: TILL THE END OF TIME, skuldnoshinpu v1.5]
row: M182
- Synthesis fuses an item into a base weapon at a workshop, spending Synthesis Materials, and passes the item's effect, its factor, to the weapon [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Synthesis] [gf:24563 §STAR OCEAN: TILL THE END OF TIME, skuldnoshinpu v1.5]
- The weapon can be synthesized again and again, so effects build up on one piece [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Synthesis] [gf:24563 §4. REFINING AND ULTIMATE WEAPONS, skuldnoshinpu v1.5]

## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Hidden
- The strongest weapons are the characters' ultimate weapons, dropped deep in the Sphere 211 dungeon with crippling negative factors that refining removes or softens [gf:24563 §4. REFINING AND ULTIMATE WEAPONS, skuldnoshinpu v1.5]

### Upgrades
- Hired Creators keep inventing on their own and report by telegraph [gf:24563 §STAR OCEAN: TILL THE END OF TIME, skuldnoshinpu v1.5]

### Shops & exchange
- The telegraph's alerts cover patents filed, items newly on sale, inventions and newly announced Creators [gf:24563 §STAR OCEAN: TILL THE END OF TIME, skuldnoshinpu v1.5]

## Unverified or contradicted

- **Patents as income.** M180 said a patented invention earns passive income as shops sell it. The wiki says the player gets money from those sales; A_I_e_x says inventing is a good way to make money under a heading too long to cite, and adds that you then buy your own invention at a fifth of the price; skuldnoshinpu describes patents without income. The row says patents put the item on sale [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Item Creation]
- **Workshops in dungeons.** The wiki places workshops in towns and dungeons; the guides read show them in towns. The row says across the world [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Item Creation]
- **Success as Talent minus Evaluation.** M180 gave the formula; no source read gives it. Cut from the row [gf:24563 §STAR OCEAN: TILL THE END OF TIME, skuldnoshinpu v1.5]
- **The best gear only invented.** M180 said the best equipment can only be invented. skuldnoshinpu says the strongest weapons are dropped in Sphere 211 and finished by refining and synthesis. Cut from the row [gf:24563 §4. REFINING AND ULTIMATE WEAPONS, skuldnoshinpu v1.5]
- **Costumes, and the exact thresholds.** The wiki lists alternate costumes among the Battle Collection's unlocks; skuldnoshinpu's Japanese-version FAQ gives 30% for Universe, 60% for the Music Test, 70% for 4D and 95% for Full Active and no costumes. The row gives the unlocks both share and no percentages [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Battle Trophy] [gf:23865 §TABLE OF CONTENTS (5 entries), skuldnoshinpu v1.6]
- **The example conditions.** M181's examples (four enemies at once, a 300-hit combo, a win on exactly 3 HP, under ten seconds, 420 enemy types) come from no source read for the US version; skuldnoshinpu's Japanese list has a 300-hit and an under-ten-seconds condition. Cut from the row [gf:23865 §2. CONDITION LISTING, skuldnoshinpu v1.6]
- **One of the first achievement systems.** The wiki says so; nothing else read does. Cut from the row [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Battle Trophy]
- **The -R suffix.** The wiki says each synthesis adds -R1, -R2 to the item's name; no guide read mentions it. Cut from the row [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Synthesis]
- **The ladder's start and the forfeited fee.** The wiki puts a new team at rank 51 and says a loss or escape forfeits the entry fee; the guide passages read do not say. Cut from the row [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Battle Arena (Star Ocean 3)]
- **Rank S's fee.** The wiki gives 5,000 Fol for the S Rank single battle; A_I_e_x gives 2,000 [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Battle Arena (Star Ocean 3)] [gf:32229 §Supreme Shop, A_I_e_x v1.00]
- **The team-battle prizes.** The wiki gives Synthesis Materials, a tri-Emblum, the Scumbag Slayer, the Star Splash and the Ring of Slaughter; A_I_e_x's list sits under an uncitable heading. g077's Ring of Slaughter row is cut [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Battle Arena (Star Ocean 3)]
- **Choosing a class.** The wiki says the player picks the class; A_I_e_x says the class changes daily and is not chosen [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Bunny Racing] [gf:32229 §Supreme Shop, A_I_e_x v1.00]
- **The two trophies' stats.** The wiki says the Bunny Race Trophy has the Victory Trophy's stats and lists them; no guide read compares them. The row no longer says so [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Bunny Racing]
