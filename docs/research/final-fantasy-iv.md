## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Hidden
- The original release's Developer's Office holds staff cameos and developers to fight, and was cut from the Easy Type, North American SNES and Pixel Remaster releases [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Developer's Office (Final Fantasy IV)]
- In the DS office a chest named Kamichi hands over a Kilobyte of Memory and takes it straight back [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Developer's Office (Final Fantasy IV 3D)]
- Perfect scores pay Whyt five outfits: Rydia's from the Intelligence game, Rosa's from Spirit, a Paladin suit from Strength, a Dragoon outfit from Stamina and a Ninja outfit from Speed [gf:51208 §Prize, MostSeriousness]

### Upgrades
- Whyt's high scores and stat boosts carry over to every other save file on the game card [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Whyt]
