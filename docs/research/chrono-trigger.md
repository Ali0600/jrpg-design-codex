## Unverified or contradicted

- **Where the stone is collected.** M192 said the stone is collected "in the present"; the guides place the recharged Sun Stone in the Sun Keep in 2300 A.D., after it is won back in 1000 A.D. The row follows them [gf:8268 §SUN KEEP (1000 AD), _ATadeo_ v1.25] [gf:55027 §The Sun Stone, Dev v1.35]
- **Frog's reforging.** M192 made the reforged Masamune part of Frog's side quest; reforging it with Dreamstone is main story, and only the upgrade at Cyrus's grave is optional. The Traded tag's quote moves to the Jerky, which the elder's wife wants from another era [gf:55027 §Melchior's Cabin (1000 A.D.), Dev v1.35] [wiki:chrono.fandom.com/Masamune]
- **Combined damage.** M193 said a Dual Tech does roughly the sum of its parts times a bonus. The wiki says so; the Mechanics Guide gives each Dual Tech its own formula from its users' stats instead. Cut [wiki:chrono.fandom.com/Tech] [gf:14148 §Dual Techs, DragonKnightZero v0.7]
- **One ending only in New Game+.** The wiki names the Frog ending as New Game+'s own; the guides say several endings need New Game+ without naming one. The row says several [wiki:chrono.fandom.com/New Game+] [gf:8268 §ENDINGS, _ATadeo_ v1.25]
- **Gato's other prizes.** _ATadeo_ gives 10 experience points and the wiki one Tech Point besides the 15 Silver Points; neither figure has a second source [gf:8268 §5. Beat Gato, _ATadeo_ v1.25] [wiki:chrono.fandom.com/Gato]
- **The soda contest.** Dev's list heads it with 5 Silver Points and then says a win pays 10; _ATadeo_ and the wiki say 5 [gf:55027 §Millennial Fair, Dev v1.35]
- **The exchange rate.** Only the wiki gives it, 10 Silver Points for 50 G. Cut from the table [wiki:chrono.fandom.com/Millennial Fair (Location)]
- **A 200-point cap.** g080 said Silver Points are capped at 200; no source read says so. Cut [gf:55027 §Millennial Fair, Dev v1.35]
- **Kittens.** The wiki says cat food lets the cats have kittens; no guide read says so [wiki:chrono.fandom.com/Norstein Bekkler]
