### Hidden
- Most common wagers draw Typhon, whose sneeze ends the match as a draw with the wager kept [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Dragon's Neck Coliseum] [web:www.cavesofnarshe.com/ff6/colosseum-guide.php]; the wiki adds that beating him pays an Elixir [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Dragon's Neck Coliseum]
