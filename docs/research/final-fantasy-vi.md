## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Hidden
- Most common wagers draw Typhon, whose sneeze ends the match as a draw with the wager kept [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Dragon's Neck Coliseum] [web:www.cavesofnarshe.com/ff6/colosseum-guide.php]; the wiki adds that beating him pays an Elixir [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Dragon's Neck Coliseum]
- Both wager tables agree that a Holy Lance and a Murakumo win each other, and that a Burning Fist wins itself back; the wiki adds that Galypdes, the Murakumo fight, carries a stealable Celestriad, and Tumbleweed a Saucer [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Dragon's Neck Coliseum] [web:www.cavesofnarshe.com/ff6/colosseum.php]
- The banquet scores the palace walkabout too: 24 soldiers count, and a perfect run of both parts reaches 93 points [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Imperial Palace (Final Fantasy VI)] [web:www.cavesofnarshe.com/ff6/walkthrough.php?chapter=1-39]
- The banquet's rewards are cumulative, and asking Gestahl the same question twice costs points (10, by Caves of Narshe's count) [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Imperial Palace (Final Fantasy VI)] [web:www.cavesofnarshe.com/ff6/walkthrough.php?chapter=1-39]

### Upgrades
- The Flame Shield and Ice Shield won from early wagers make otherwise unwinnable coliseum fights survivable, the wiki suggests [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Dragon's Neck Coliseum]

### Shops & exchange
- A unique lot the party passes up comes back at a later auction until it is bought [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Auction House (Final Fantasy VI)] [web:www.cavesofnarshe.com/ff6/walkthrough.php?chapter=1-36]
- With neither esper bought, a World of Balance auction shows Angel Wings half the time, the chocobo a quarter, Golem 12.5% and Zona Seeker and the Angel Ring 6.25% each [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Auction House (Final Fantasy VI)] [web:www.cavesofnarshe.com/ff6/walkthrough.php?chapter=1-36]
- The Zephyr Cloak costs 7,000 from a merchant in Nikeah, against 10,000 at auction [web:www.cavesofnarshe.com/ff6/walkthrough.php?chapter=2-13]

## Unverified or contradicted
- **The Ichigeki without Shadow.** The wiki says the wager falls back to Typhon for an Elixir; Caves of Narshe's list gives Typhon with the Ichigeki as the prize [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Dragon's Neck Coliseum] [web:www.cavesofnarshe.com/ff6/colosseum.php]
- **The banquet's top boundary.** The wiki's tiers are 77–89 and 90–93; Caves of Narshe labels them 77–90 and 90–93 [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Imperial Palace (Final Fantasy VI)] [web:www.cavesofnarshe.com/ff6/walkthrough.php?chapter=1-39]
- **Excalipoor at auction.** The wiki says only the Game Boy Advance release sells it (500,000 gil, after an NPC mentions it); Caves of Narshe's Pixel Remaster walkthrough includes it in the World of Ruin script [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Auction House (Final Fantasy VI)] [web:www.cavesofnarshe.com/ff6/walkthrough.php?chapter=2-13]
