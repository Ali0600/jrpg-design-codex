## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Hidden
- The original NES release pays only 100 gil for a solve; the WonderSwan Color and PlayStation releases pay 10,000, 5,000 and 2,000 gil for the three fastest places [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/15 Puzzle]
- On the Game Boy Advance the puzzle opens by pressing A and B together 22 times aboard the ship [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/15 Puzzle]
- The Pixel Remaster drops the timer and the prizes, and grants an achievement for starting the puzzle [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/15 Puzzle]
- A top-three finish also pays a finishing prize, by the wiki's account [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/15 Puzzle]
