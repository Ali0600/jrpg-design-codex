## Mechanics candidates

The seven existing rows, sourced on 2026-09-16. No new rows: M058–M064 are edited in place.

### M058 Fun-Size Forge (crafting as a minigame)
pointers: [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Fun-Size Forge] [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Perfectionist's pearl] [gf:76716 §Fun-Size Forge, AzMuch v1.30]
row: M058
- The forge is used at a campsite, and forging an item is a minigame: the hero strikes each section of the piece to bring its gauge into a target zone [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Fun-Size Forge] [gf:76716 §Fun-Size Forge, AzMuch v1.30]
- Every strike spends Focus, and a basic bash costs 5; the forging session ends when too little Focus is left [gf:76716 §Fun-Size Forge, AzMuch v1.30]
- As the hero levels up he learns Flourishes, special strikes such as a half-strength strike or one that hits several squares [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Fun-Size Forge] [gf:76716 §Fun-Size Forge, AzMuch v1.30]; the first, Double-Edged Bash, comes at level 10 [gf:76716 §Fun-Size Forge, AzMuch v1.30]
- A well-forged item comes out +1 to +3, and a success pays Perfectionist's Pearls, more for a better result [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Perfectionist's pearl] [gf:76716 §Fun-Size Forge, AzMuch v1.30]
- Pearls rework an item, including one found or bought, for another try at a higher grade, up to +3 [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Perfectionist's pearl] [gf:76716 §Fun-Size Forge, AzMuch v1.30]
- A failed forging still makes the item, at standard quality and with no pearls [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Fun-Size Forge]

### M059 Recipe books hidden in the world
pointers: [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Fun-Size Forge] [gf:76716 §Recipe Books, AzMuch v1.30]
row: M059
- An item cannot be forged until its recipe has been found [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Fun-Size Forge] [gf:76716 §Fun-Size Forge, AzMuch v1.30]
- Recipes come from books on bookshelves, in treasure chests and as quest rewards [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Fun-Size Forge] [gf:76716 §Fun-Size Forge, AzMuch v1.30]
- Most books hold several recipes: So You Want to Be a Witch, on a bookshelf in the Gallopolis inn, teaches five, from the Lamp Post to Enchantress's Gloves [gf:76716 §Recipe Books, AzMuch v1.30]

### M060 Skill panels (character builder grids)
pointers: [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Dragon Quest XI: Echoes of an Elusive Age] [gf:76716 §Character Builder, AzMuch v1.30]
row: M060
- Skill points are spent in the Character Builder on panels of spells, abilities and attributes, with a tree for each weapon type the character can use [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Dragon Quest XI: Echoes of an Elusive Age] [gf:76716 §Character Builder, AzMuch v1.30]
- A panel can be learned only next to one already learned [gf:76716 §Character Builder, AzMuch v1.30]; learning a panel makes the ones around it available [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Dragon Quest XI: Echoes of an Elusive Age]
- Secret panels unlock once the panels around them are learned [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Dragon Quest XI: Echoes of an Elusive Age] [gf:76716 §Character Builder, AzMuch v1.30]
- Spent points can be recovered and spent again: Rectification at a church or statue costs 20 gold a point [gf:76716 §Character Builder, AzMuch v1.30], and the Definitive Edition allows a Skill Reset in the Character Builder at any time [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Dragon Quest XI: Echoes of an Elusive Age]

### M061 Pep powers (state-based team moves)
pointers: [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Pep Power] [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Pep Up] [gf:76716 §TACTICS, AzMuch v1.30]
row: M061
- In battle a character can become Pepped Up, a temporary state [gf:76716 §TACTICS, AzMuch v1.30] [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Pep Up]; the Luminary's Pep Up skill forces it for 4 to 6 turns [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Pep Up]
- Pep powers are combination moves that need specific characters pepped up at once [gf:76716 §TACTICS, AzMuch v1.30] [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Pep Power]; Chop 'n' Churn needs the Luminary and Serena, Gigaburst the Luminary and three allies [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Pep Power]
- AzMuch adds that pep comes most often after repeated enemy attacks, raises stats that differ per character, carries into the next battle and ends when a pep power is used [gf:76716 §TACTICS, AzMuch v1.30]

### M062 Tickington / Tockles (retro world collect-a-thon)
pointers: [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Tickington] [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Tockle] [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Dragon Quest XI: Echoes of an Elusive Age] [gf:78060 §I. What is Tickington?, DarkAncient2 v1.0.0]
row: M062
- Tickington exists only in the 3DS and Definitive Edition versions [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Tickington] [gf:78060 §I. What is Tickington?, DarkAncient2 v1.0.0]
- Its Echo Chamber holds ten altars, one for each of the first ten mainline games [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Tickington] [gf:78060 §I. What is Tickington?, DarkAncient2 v1.0.0]
- Tockles sent out into the world hold pastwords, and each pastword opens a realm from a past game [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Tockle] [gf:78060 §II. Pastword Tockle Locations, DarkAncient2 v1.0.0]; the guide counts 25 pastword Tockles [gf:78060 §II. Pastword Tockle Locations, DarkAncient2 v1.0.0]
- The realms are a 2D world [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Dragon Quest XI: Echoes of an Elusive Age], and each holds quests given by its townsfolk [gf:78060 §Altar of Origins (Dragon Quest 1), DarkAncient2 v1.0.0]
- Clearing every Tickington quest opens a final altar [gf:78060 §IV. Altar of an Elusive Age, DarkAncient2 v1.0.0]
- Only the Luminary can see Tockles at first [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Tockle]

### M063 Draconian Quests (self-imposed challenge toggles)
pointers: [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Draconian Quest] [gf:76716 §Other Features, AzMuch v1.30]
row: M063
- Draconian Quest restrictions are chosen when a new adventure log is started [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Draconian Quest] [gf:76716 §Other Features, AzMuch v1.30]
- The original release offers six: no fleeing, no shopping, no armour, reduced experience from easy fights, super-strong enemies and Shypox [gf:76716 §Other Features, AzMuch v1.30] [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Draconian Quest]; the Definitive Edition adds three more and drops no fleeing [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Draconian Quest]
- Shypox makes the hero hesitate at random, losing a turn or failing to talk to someone [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Draconian Quest] [gf:76716 §Other Features, AzMuch v1.30]
- A restriction can be removed later but not added back [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Draconian Quest]; AzMuch says the removal is done at a church [gf:76716 §Other Features, AzMuch v1.30]
- Finishing with a restriction on puts a star beside the save file [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Draconian Quest]

### M064 Monster mounts (enemies as traversal keys)
pointers: [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Mount (Dragon Quest XI)] [gf:76716 §GETTING AROUND, AzMuch v1.30] [gf:78060 §II. Pastword Tockle Locations, DarkAncient2 v1.0.0]
row: M064
- Some monsters can be ridden once defeated [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Mount (Dragon Quest XI)] [gf:76716 §GETTING AROUND, AzMuch v1.30]
- Each mount's trait helps with the area where it is found, and a mount cannot leave that area [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Mount (Dragon Quest XI)] [gf:76716 §GETTING AROUND, AzMuch v1.30]
- The traits by genus: Demonrider gallops and climbs walls, Eggsoskeleton jumps, Horknight jumps and hovers over water, Face invader charges through lesser monsters and blockades, Dragon rider flies and glides, Headless horseman gallops [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Mount (Dragon Quest XI)]
- A dragon mount is needed to reach Insula Algarum, where one of the pastword Tockles waits [gf:78060 §II. Pastword Tockle Locations, DarkAncient2 v1.0.0] [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Mount (Dragon Quest XI)]
- A mountable monster shows a golden sheen in the field and is a fixed encounter [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Mount (Dragon Quest XI)]

## Unverified or contradicted
- **Secret panels need four neighbours or all of them.** AzMuch says four of the surrounding panels; the wiki's game article says all of them. The row says only "the panels around them" [gf:76716 §Character Builder, AzMuch v1.30] [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Dragon Quest XI: Echoes of an Elusive Age]
- **A hex grid, and cheap respec.** M060 called the board a hex grid and the reset cheap. Neither source describes the board's shape, and the one price found, 20 gold a point, is not called cheap, so both words are cut [gf:76716 §Character Builder, AzMuch v1.30] [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Dragon Quest XI: Echoes of an Elusive Age]
- **Pep comes randomly after enough turns.** M061 said so. AzMuch says it comes occasionally, most often after repeated enemy attacks, and the wiki gives no trigger, so the row names none [gf:76716 §TACTICS, AzMuch v1.30] [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Pep Power]
- **Pep powers required for quests or rare-item farming.** M061 said some are. Neither source says so, and the clause is cut [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Pep Power] [gf:76716 §TACTICS, AzMuch v1.30]
- **Tickington rewards flowing back into the main game.** M062 said so. The Tickington guide says each quest pays a reward without naming one, and the wiki names none, so the clause is cut [gf:78060 §Altar of Origins (Dragon Quest 1), DarkAncient2 v1.0.0] [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Tickington]
- **Tockles as invisible spirits.** The wiki says people other than the Luminary cannot see them; the guide does not say it. The row calls them hidden, which both support [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Tockle] [gf:78060 §II. Pastword Tockle Locations, DarkAncient2 v1.0.0]
- **Mounts as glowing monsters, each needed for treasures.** M064 said the mounts glow and guard specific treasures. The golden sheen is on the wiki alone, and neither source ties mounts to treasure; both say a mount gets past obstacles in its own area, and the guide names one place a dragon mount reaches. The row now says "places" [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Mount (Dragon Quest XI)] [gf:76716 §GETTING AROUND, AzMuch v1.30] [gf:78060 §II. Pastword Tockle Locations, DarkAncient2 v1.0.0]
- **Wall-climbing and smashing mounts.** Only the wiki's genus table lists the traits; the row keeps the dragon's flight, which the Tickington guide also needs, and states the rest generally [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Mount (Dragon Quest XI)] [gf:78060 §II. Pastword Tockle Locations, DarkAncient2 v1.0.0]
