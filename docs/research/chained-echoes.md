### M072 Reward Board (achievements that pay gear)
pointers: [gf:80256 §SYSTEM EXPLANATION: REWARD BOARD, Bkstunt_31 v1.20] [web:steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2899301183] [web:gamerant.com/beginner-tips-chained-echoes] [wiki:chainedechoes.fandom.com/Rusty Weapons]
row: M072
- Each field on the Reward Board is a task whose reward is claimed once it is done, and entering certain areas unlocks new parts of the board [gf:80256 §SYSTEM EXPLANATION: REWARD BOARD, Bkstunt_31 v1.20] [web:steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2899301183]
- Finished fields next to each other form a chain, and the longest chain pays extra rewards [gf:80256 §SYSTEM EXPLANATION: REWARD BOARD, Bkstunt_31 v1.20] [web:steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2899301183]; the Knuckles come at a chain of 96 [wiki:chainedechoes.fandom.com/Rusty Weapons]
- Game Rant calls it an achievement system, with tasks such as opening a number of chests or exploring part of an area [web:gamerant.com/beginner-tips-chained-echoes]
- Unique monsters are tasks on it [web:www.thegamer.com/chained-echoes-shop-deals-ultimate-armor-guide] [gf:80256 §SYSTEM EXPLANATION: REWARD BOARD, Bkstunt_31 v1.20]
- Game Rant lists a Grimoire Shard for chains of 8, 24, 40, 56, 64, 80, 88 and 104 [web:gamerant.com/chained-echoes-grimoire-shard-guide-how-to-earn-spend]

### M073 Grimoire Shards instead of levels (fixed growth)
pointers: [gf:80256 §CHARACTER PROGRESSION I-IV, Bkstunt_31 v1.20] [web:gamerant.com/beginner-tips-chained-echoes] [web:gamerant.com/chained-echoes-level-up-fast] [wiki:chainedechoes.fandom.com/Skills]
row: M073
- There is no traditional level system; characters grow through Grimoire Shards [web:gamerant.com/beginner-tips-chained-echoes] [gf:80256 §CHARACTER PROGRESSION I-IV, Bkstunt_31 v1.20]
- Shards come from bosses and other set pieces [gf:80256 §CHARACTER PROGRESSION I-IV, Bkstunt_31 v1.20] [wiki:chainedechoes.fandom.com/Skills] [web:gamerant.com/chained-echoes-level-up-fast], and one shard serves every party member [gf:80256 §CHARACTER PROGRESSION I-IV, Bkstunt_31 v1.20]
- A shard buys an action skill, a passive skill or a stat boost [gf:80256 §CHARACTER PROGRESSION I-IV, Bkstunt_31 v1.20] [web:gamerant.com/chained-echoes-grimoire-shard-guide-how-to-earn-spend]
- Battles pay SP, which levels the skills a character has equipped [gf:80256 §CHARACTER PROGRESSION I-IV, Bkstunt_31 v1.20] [web:gamerant.com/chained-echoes-level-up-fast]
- Bkstunt_31 calls the shards the game's way of keeping the party from being overleveled [gf:80256 §CONCLUSION, Bkstunt_31 v1.20]

### M074 Overdrive bar (combat tempo management)
pointers: [gf:80256 §SYSTEM EXPLANATION: OVERDRIVE I - IV, Bkstunt_31 v1.20] [web:steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2899301183] [wiki:chainedechoes.fandom.com/Combat] [web:gamerant.com/beginner-tips-chained-echoes]
row: M074
- The Overdrive bar has yellow, green and red areas showing the party's state, and almost every action moves its cursor right [gf:80256 §SYSTEM EXPLANATION: OVERDRIVE I - IV, Bkstunt_31 v1.20] [web:steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2899301183]
- In the green area the party deals more damage, takes less and pays half the TP for skills [web:steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2899301183] [gf:80256 §SYSTEM EXPLANATION: OVERDRIVE I - IV, Bkstunt_31 v1.20] [web:gamerant.com/beginner-tips-chained-echoes]; the game's data puts it at 1.25 times the damage dealt and 0.85 times the damage taken [wiki:chainedechoes.fandom.com/Combat]
- In the red the party takes much more damage [gf:80256 §SYSTEM EXPLANATION: OVERDRIVE I - IV, Bkstunt_31 v1.20] [wiki:chainedechoes.fandom.com/Combat], 1.25 times physical and 1.5 times magical [wiki:chainedechoes.fandom.com/Combat]
- A skill of the type shown beside the bar, or switching a character, moves the cursor back left [gf:80256 §SYSTEM EXPLANATION: OVERDRIVE I - IV, Bkstunt_31 v1.20] [web:steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2899301183]; Bkstunt_31 adds Defend and Ultra Moves [gf:80256 §SYSTEM EXPLANATION: OVERDRIVE I - IV, Bkstunt_31 v1.20]

### M075 Crystal socketing + upgrades that add slots
pointers: [gf:80256 §SYSTEM EXPLANATION: CRAFTING I-V, Bkstunt_31 v1.20] [web:steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2899301183] [web:gamerant.com/chained-echoes-crystals-guide-fusion-combine-purity-rank-size]
row: M075
- Weapons and armor are upgraded twice, shown as + and ++, with materials from chests or monsters; each upgrade raises stats and adds slots for crystals [gf:80256 §SYSTEM EXPLANATION: CRAFTING I-V, Bkstunt_31 v1.20] [web:steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2899301183]
- A crystal carries a passive skill, which works once the crystal reaches rank III and grows stronger at ranks V and X [gf:80256 §SYSTEM EXPLANATION: CRAFTING I-V, Bkstunt_31 v1.20] [web:gamerant.com/chained-echoes-crystals-guide-fusion-combine-purity-rank-size]

### M076 Sky Armor (mechs as traversal + second combat layer)
pointers: [gf:80256 §SKY ARMOR PROGRESSION, Bkstunt_31 v1.20] [gf:80256 §Sky Armor Battles IV, Bkstunt_31 v1.20] [web:steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2984880464]
row: M076
- Whole areas are Sky Armor territory, and the mechs fly across gaps [gf:80256 §THOPAS THE MINER, Bkstunt_31 v1.20] [gf:80256 §SKY ARMOR PROGRESSION, Bkstunt_31 v1.20]; the Steam guide treats them as Sky Armor sections [web:steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2984880464]
- Some chests can only be reached in a mech [gf:80256 §VESTA THE CARTOGRAPHER, Bkstunt_31 v1.20], and chests in the Sky Armor sections are the only source of Sky Armor upgrades for most of the game [web:steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2984880464]
- In a Sky Armor battle the mech switches gears instead of party members: Gear 2 deals and takes more damage at a higher TP cost, and Gear 0 uses no skills but restores TP [gf:80256 §Sky Armor Battles IV, Bkstunt_31 v1.20]; the Steam guide shifts gears to recover TP or move the Overdrive gauge [web:steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2984880464]

- RAM parts, such as Status Resist RAM I, turn up in chests [gf:80256 §VESTA THE CARTOGRAPHER, Bkstunt_31 v1.20] [web:steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2984880464]

### M077 Vendor Deals board (targeted hunting shop)
pointers: [gf:80256 §SYSTEM EXPLANATION: DEALS I-II, Bkstunt_31 v1.20] [web:steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2899301183] [web:gamerant.com/chained-echoes-deals-guide-how-to-unlock-and-buy] [web:www.thegamer.com/chained-echoes-shop-deals-ultimate-armor-guide]

- The Deals list shows each deal's remaining items [gf:80256 §SYSTEM EXPLANATION: DEALS I-II, Bkstunt_31 v1.20] [web:www.thegamer.com/chained-echoes-shop-deals-ultimate-armor-guide], and progress is shared by every merchant [web:gamerant.com/chained-echoes-deals-guide-how-to-unlock-and-buy] [web:www.thegamer.com/chained-echoes-shop-deals-ultimate-armor-guide]

## Unverified or contradicted
- **Deals that say where and from what enemy.** M077 said each deal names where its items come from. TheGamer says a deal lists only the items, and that recruiting Tehlla is what shows where materials drop; Bkstunt_31's list gives items and rewards only. The claim is cut [web:www.thegamer.com/chained-echoes-shop-deals-ultimate-armor-guide] [gf:80256 §DEALS, Bkstunt_31 v1.20]
- **A guild reward for defeating every unique monster, tied to ultimate equipment.** M078 said so. Game Rant says guild leaders pay per hunt, TheGamer ties the Ultimate armor deals to unique monster loot, and no source names a prize for all of them. The row keeps the board and the hints; payment per hunt rests on Game Rant alone [web:gamerant.com/chained-echoes-how-to-join-the-adventurers-guild] [web:www.thegamer.com/chained-echoes-shop-deals-ultimate-armor-guide]
- **No random encounters, all enemies visible.** M073's name and text said so. No source read states it, so the name and the clause are cut [gf:80256 §CHARACTER PROGRESSION I-IV, Bkstunt_31 v1.20] [web:gamerant.com/beginner-tips-chained-echoes]
- **Grinding neither needed nor possible.** M073 said so. Bkstunt_31 says shards keep the party from being overleveled, but Game Rant's levelling guide gives ways to farm SP faster, so the row says only where growth comes from [gf:80256 §CONCLUSION, Bkstunt_31 v1.20] [web:gamerant.com/chained-echoes-level-up-fast]
- **Crystals refined smaller and stronger.** M075 said crystals are refined; the sources describe fusion, which sums ranks and averages sizes, and the row now says that [web:gamerant.com/chained-echoes-crystals-guide-fusion-combine-purity-rank-size] [gf:80256 §SYSTEM EXPLANATION: CRAFTING I-V, Bkstunt_31 v1.20]
- **Mid-game, each character gains a mech with fast overworld traversal and Skill RAM bought or found.** M076 said so. The sources support flying Sky Armor areas, chests and found RAM parts, not a timing per character, overworld speed or an item called Skill RAM; the Steam guide says Sky Armor upgrades are not sold until very late [web:steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2984880464] [gf:80256 §SKY ARMOR PROGRESSION, Bkstunt_31 v1.20]
- **Board tiles paying gear, materials and SP, and bonuses for rows and clusters.** M072 said so.
