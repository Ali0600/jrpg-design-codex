## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Hidden
- Vyv's sixth commission, A Place to Call Home, also pays the Time photo filter [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Vyv Dorden]
- The 10-gil machines also pay healing items below 20 chests (a Potion at 2, a Hi-Potion at 5, an Elixir at 10, a Hi-Elixir at 15) and a Mega Phoenix at 40; the Altissia machine pays a Hi-Potion at 5, a Mega Phoenix at 10 and the Wind-up Lord Vexxos, a treasure that sells for 500,000 gil, at 99 [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Justice Monsters Five]
- The same tables list a Titanium Bangle at 35 and a Gold Bangle at 50 chests on the 10-gil machines; the XV wiki's entries for those two bangles name no pinball source [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Justice Monsters Five] [wiki:finalfantasyxv.fandom.com/Accessory]

### Upgrades
- Four more races pay medals the XV wiki has no page for: the Corsa Medal for beating Ignis, the Fat Chocobo Medal for the Full Field race, the Long Course Medal for the Rocky Road hoops course, and the Jennet Medal for beating Iris from Chapter 8 [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Chocobo Races (Final Fantasy XV)]
- Medals adorn the chocobo, fruit found along the tracks unlocks new colours at the Wiz Chocobo Post's salon, and two Ascension abilities pay 1 AP or 50 EXP per race won [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Chocobo Races (Final Fantasy XV)]
- Navyth's four quests also pay 1,500, 2,000, 3,000 and 5,000 EXP in turn, and each unlocks the next [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Navyth Arlund] [wiki:finalfantasyxv.fandom.com/Sidequest]
- Noctis's fishing level rises from 1 to 10, lowering tackle-shop prices by 10%, 30% and then 50% at levels 3, 6 and 9 [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Fishing (Final Fantasy XV)]

### Shops & exchange
- Four of the prize counter's one-time items give way to a second item at the same price once bought [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Totomostro] [gf:74357 §Prizes, elduende05 v0.8]
- The Magitek Generator gives way to the Onion Bangle at a lower price, 2,400,000 medals [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Totomostro] [wiki:finalfantasyxv.fandom.com/Accessory]
- Pinball prizes can be won again on every game, which makes the Altissia machine a repeatable source of Moogle Charms, an accessory placed only five times in the world [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Justice Monsters Five]

## Unverified or contradicted
- **The Oracle Earring's threshold.** The Final Fantasy Wiki's table puts it at 60 chests on the 10-gil machines; the XV wiki says 55 [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Justice Monsters Five] [wiki:finalfantasyxv.fandom.com/Accessory]
- **Cooking levels 1 and 2.** The XV wiki's level table gives Prairie-Style Skewers at level 1 and Croque Madame at level 2, while its own recipe list says level 2 for both; elduende05 lists Croque Madame at skill 1 and Prairie-Style Skewers at skill 2 [wiki:finalfantasyxv.fandom.com/Cooking] [gf:74356 §Page 1, elduende05 v0.9]
- **What Able Angler and Ace Angler do.** The wiki says the two fishing levels raise the largest catchable fish to 31.5 and 59 inches; elduende05 says they show medium- and high-level fish on the minimap [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Fishing (Final Fantasy XV)] [gf:74355 §Q: What's the hardest fish to catch?, elduende05 v0.91]
- **The Exhaust Manifold.** The wiki lists it at 480,000 medals in the Windows and Royal Edition releases only; the guide predates those releases [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Totomostro] [gf:74357 §Prizes, elduende05 v0.8]
