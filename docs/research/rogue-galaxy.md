## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Hidden
- Thinking Circles are the game's general spot for using a key item, not only for quarries: an early one takes the Power Glove to move a statue [gf:48402 §/ Version History \, wizardmentor v2.02]

### Upgrades
- After a weapon is Maxed it keeps gaining elemental damage until it is Mastered; MaloFresk agrees under an uncitable heading [wiki:roguegalaxy.fandom.com/Toady]

### Shops & exchange
- The first two quarries' information costs 800 Zehn each [gf:48402 §/ Version History \, wizardmentor v2.02]

## Unverified or contradicted

- **Mastered, and building up before fusing.** M172 said a Maxed weapon keeps building elemental damage until Mastered, and that the more is built up before fusing the better the result. The wiki says both; MaloFresk confirms Mastered only under a heading too long to cite, and says the elemental shards are not worth the time. Cut from the row [wiki:roguegalaxy.fandom.com/Toady]
- **Toady's warnings.** The wiki says Toady warns when a combination would be useless; MaloFresk says only that he must like the combination. The row says he accepts only some [wiki:roguegalaxy.fandom.com/Toady] [gf:46872 §Q: What's this all about?, MaloFresk v1.21]
- **A galaxy-wide rank and a kill quota.** M173 called the Hunter Rank galaxy-wide and described a Hunting Record with a kill quota for every monster. vhayste calls the rank a top-100 table under an uncitable heading, wizardmentor's Battle Log counts enemies dispatched, and the wiki's Hunting Record page is empty. Cut from the row [gf:48402 §/ Version History \, wizardmentor v2.02]
- **Rare gear from every quarry.** The wiki says quarries drop rare and valuable items; wizardmentor's first quarry drops a Tri-Heal, a Resurrection and 280 Zehn. The row says drops [wiki:roguegalaxy.fandom.com/Quarries] [gf:48402 §/ Version History \, wizardmentor v2.02]
- **Rank B's prize.** The wiki gives Devil Forks for Rank B's first win; vhayste's table gives the Spirit Calibur. Neither goes in the table [wiki:roguegalaxy.fandom.com/Insector] [gf:48466 §E 400 Battle Feed x5 (5 entries), vhayste v3.0]
- **Rank S's first and third prizes.** The wiki gives the Demon Medium for the first Rank S win and a Perfect Elixir for the third; vhayste's table leaves Rank S's prize blank. Cut from the table [wiki:roguegalaxy.fandom.com/Insector]
- **Feeding spends lifespan.** The wiki says an Insector's lifespan is spent in points by feeding and the rest goes into stats; no guide read says so. Cut from the row [wiki:roguegalaxy.fandom.com/Insector]
- **130-odd Insectors.** The wiki counts 136 besides the Dark Emperor and White Miracle; no guide read gives a count. The row no longer does [wiki:roguegalaxy.fandom.com/Insector]
- **Twenty-two quarries.** Jaymieman counts 22; the wiki's list was not counted. The row gives no number [gf:47939 §Quarries 101 (quarr1), Jaymieman v1.0]
