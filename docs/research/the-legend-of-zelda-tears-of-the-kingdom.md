### M065 Shrines → Lights of Blessing economy
pointers: [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Light of Blessing] [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/412274] [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/411058]
row: M065
- Clearing a shrine pays one Light of Blessing [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Light of Blessing] [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/412274]
- Four Lights of Blessing are traded at a Goddess Statue for either a Heart Container or a Stamina Vessel [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Light of Blessing] [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/412274]
- There are 152 shrines [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/411058] [gf:80705 §Target Numbers and Notes, cmbaum v3.0], 120 on the surface and 32 in the sky [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/411058]; the wiki counts 152 Lights and 38 upgrades [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Light of Blessing]

### M066 Korok seeds → inventory expansion
pointers: [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Korok Seed] [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/411610] [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/409120] [gf:80705 §Target Numbers and Notes, cmbaum v3.0]
row: M066
- There are 1,000 Korok Seeds [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Korok Seed] [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/409120] [gf:80705 §Target Numbers and Notes, cmbaum v3.0]
- Seeds come from Koroks found by solving small puzzles in the landscape, such as lifting a rock from an odd spot or completing a circle of rocks [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/411610] [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/409120]; the wiki says Koroks hand them over when found throughout Hyrule [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Korok Seed]
- Hestu trades seeds for weapon, bow and shield inventory slots [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Korok Seed] [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/411610]
- Every slot costs 421 seeds in all [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Korok Seed] [gf:80705 §Target Numbers and Notes, cmbaum v3.0]; Game8 says 440, for 20 weapon, 20 shield and 14 bow slots [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/411610]

### M067 Armor hunting + Great Fairy upgrades
pointers: [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Great Fairy] [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Koltin] [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/412535] [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/412652] [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/412378]
row: M067
- Armor is collected across the world, 136 unique pieces in all [gf:80705 §Target Numbers and Notes, cmbaum v3.0], in 36 sets [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/412378]
- Wearing a set together can add a set bonus, such as the Mystic Set's rupees lost in place of hearts [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Koltin] [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/412378]; Game8 says a bonus activates only once every piece is upgraded to level 2 [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/412652]
- The four Great Fairies hide in their flowers until a questline brings musicians from the stables to play for them [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Great Fairy] [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/412535]
- A Great Fairy upgrades armor for materials plus rupees [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Great Fairy] [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/412652]
- Each fairy unlocked raises the level armor can reach, up to four [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/412652]

### M068 Caves + Bubbulfrogs (layered cave economy)
pointers: [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Bubbulfrog] [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Koltin] [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/411749] [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/411111] [gf:80705 §Target Numbers and Notes, cmbaum v3.0]
row: M068
- Bubbulfrogs live only in caves, and a defeated one drops a Bubbul Gem [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Bubbulfrog] [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/411111]
- Game8 counts 147 caves with one Bubbulfrog each [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/411749], and cmbaum counts 147 Bubbul Gems [gf:80705 §Target Numbers and Notes, cmbaum v3.0]
- Koltin trades the gems for enemy masks (Moblin, Lizalfos, Horriblin, Lynel) and the Mystic Set [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Koltin] [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/411111]
- Bringing him every gem ends his quest: 45 gems cover the masks and the Mystic Set, and Koltin's Fabric for the paraglider comes after the rest [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/411111]
- A cave whose gem has been taken gets a checkmark on the map [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/411111]

- The Depths are an underground level mirroring the surface [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Depths]; every Lightroot has a shrine on the surface above it [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Lightroot] [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/412609]

- An activated Lightroot lights the area around it and becomes a fast-travel point [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Lightroot] [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/412609]
- A Lightroot's name is its shrine's name spelled backwards, and activating one reveals part of the map [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Lightroot] [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Depths]

## Unverified or contradicted
- **Korok seeds for a full inventory: 421 or 440.** The wiki and cmbaum say 421; Game8's slot table adds up to 440. The row follows the two that agree [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Korok Seed] [gf:80705 §Target Numbers and Notes, cmbaum v3.0] [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/411610]
- **Bubbul Gems: 146 or 147.** Game8's gem guide says 146, its cave list and cmbaum 147. The row follows 147 [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/411111] [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/411749] [gf:80705 §Target Numbers and Notes, cmbaum v3.0]
- **What 100 Crystallized Charges buy.** The wiki says a whole new Energy Cell; Game8 says one Energy Well, a third of a cell. The row says "more battery capacity" [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Energy Cell] [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/411118]
- **A traveling merchant.** M068 called Koltin one, and `docs/verbs.md` tagged the row *The wanderer* for it. Only Game8 lists his several night-time spots; the wiki says nothing of where he stands, so the word and the tag are dropped [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/413877] [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Koltin]
- **Exclusive outfits, and cave entrances marked once cleared.** M068 said so. Neither source calls Koltin's stock exclusive, and only Game8 mentions the map checkmark, so both are cut [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/411111] [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Koltin]
- **Discovering a Lightroot hints its shrine.** M069 said either layer hints the other. Game8 says a found shrine points to the Lightroot below; neither source says the reverse, so the row says only that [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/412609] [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Lightroot]
- **Charges from mining, sky islands and dispensers.** M070 named those three. The sources give Forge Constructs, minibosses, temple bosses and Yiga treasure rooms, not sky islands or dispensers, so the list is cut [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/412271] [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Crystallized Charge]
- **Hearts or stamina "at goddess statues — the player chooses".** Kept; both sources say the four Lights buy one or the other [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Light of Blessing] [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/412274]
- **Armor in caves, quests and chests; fairies upgrading with monster materials.** M067 said so. Game8's set list names a cave for one set and the wiki names Koltin's trades, but neither lists those three sources or says the materials come from monsters, so the row says "materials" and "across the world" [web:game8.co/games/Zelda-Tears-of-the-Kingdom/archives/412378] [wiki:zelda.fandom.com/Great Fairy]
