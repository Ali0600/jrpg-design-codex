### M122 Shard hunting (exploration assembles the world)
pointers: [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Dragon Quest VII] [gf:14558 §5A. Shards, Shdwwrym v4.0]
row: M122
- The world first seems to be one small island, Estard [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Dragon Quest VII] [gf:14558 §5A. Shards, Shdwwrym v4.0]
- Stone shards found on the journey go on pedestals in the Shrine of Mysteries (the Fane), and a completed pedestal revives a sealed land [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Dragon Quest VII] [gf:14558 §5A. Shards, Shdwwrym v4.0]
- The party is sent to that land's past, solves its trouble, and the land then appears in the present [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Dragon Quest VII] [gf:14558 §5A. Shards, Shdwwrym v4.0]

### M123 Vocation system (classes mastered and hybridized)
pointers: [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Dragon Quest VII] [gf:14558 §4J. Human Classes, Shdwwrym v4.0] [gf:14558 §4K. Monster Classes, Shdwwrym v4.0]
row: M123
- Characters change class at the temple of vocations, Dharma Temple in this guide and Alltrades Abbey on the wiki, and gain class levels by fighting battles [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Dragon Quest VII] [gf:14558 §4J. Human Classes, Shdwwrym v4.0]
- Mastering certain combinations of classes unlocks further classes [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Dragon Quest VII] [gf:14558 §4J. Human Classes, Shdwwrym v4.0]
- A monster class needs that monster's heart, found in chests or dropped by the monster [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Dragon Quest VII] [gf:14558 §4K. Monster Classes, Shdwwrym v4.0]
- Spells and skills learned stay usable in other classes, and combination skills come from maturing in two classes in order [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Dragon Quest VII] [gf:14558 §4J. Human Classes, Shdwwrym v4.0]

### M124 Immigrant Town (recruit settlers, evolve a city)
pointers: [gf:14558 §5D. Immigrant Town, Shdwwrym v4.0] [gf:15425 §Section 3: Town phases, Evanidus v1.6] [gf:15425 §Section 5: Possible Final Forms, Evanidus v1.6]
row: M124
- An old man, Sim, on an empty field wants to found a town, and people met around the world can be sent to it [gf:14558 §5D. Immigrant Town, Shdwwrym v4.0] [gf:15425 §Section 1: The Immigrant Town, Evanidus v1.6]
- The town changes form as its population passes set numbers, gaining a name, houses, shops, an inn and a church [gf:14558 §5D. Immigrant Town, Shdwwrym v4.0] [gf:15425 §Section 3: Town phases, Evanidus v1.6]
- From 35 residents it reaches a final form, which differs with the kinds of people invited [gf:14558 §5D. Immigrant Town, Shdwwrym v4.0] [gf:15425 §Section 5: Possible Final Forms, Evanidus v1.6]
