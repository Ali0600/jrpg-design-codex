### M148 Tools (Field Gadgets)
pointers: [wiki:wildarms.fandom.com/Tools] [wiki:wildarms.fandom.com/Radar] [gf:21324 §The Final Confrontation, DavidK5 v9.0]
row: M148
- Each character carries their own set of tools, found in dungeons or handed over at events, and automatically given to their owner [wiki:wildarms.fandom.com/Tools] [gf:21324 §The Final Confrontation, DavidK5 v9.0]
- To use one you switch the party leader to the tool's owner and pick the tool [wiki:wildarms.fandom.com/Tools]
- Some tools are required to proceed; others only open optional content [wiki:wildarms.fandom.com/Tools] [gf:21324 §The Final Confrontation, DavidK5 v9.0]
- The twelve in this game, four each: Rudy's Bombs, Radar, Skates and Power Glove; Jack's Hanpan, Lighter, Grappling Hook and Guitar; Cecilia's Tear Drop, Pocket Watch, Wand and Vase [gf:21324 §The Final Confrontation, DavidK5 v9.0]
- The Radar detects items, Gella and locations on the world map [wiki:wildarms.fandom.com/Radar] [gf:21324 §The Final Confrontation, DavidK5 v9.0]
- A tool is tied to its character and goes with them if they leave the party, which is why guests carry none [wiki:wildarms.fandom.com/Tools]
- Worked examples: the Grappling Hook crosses pits in the Pleasing Garden, the Pocket Watch resets a room's puzzle at Curan Abbey [gf:21324 §Pleasing Garden, DavidK5 v9.0] [gf:21324 §A Girl in an Abbey, DavidK5 v9.0]

### M149 Crest Sorcery (Crest Graphs)
pointers: [wiki:wildarms.fandom.com/Crest Sorcery] [gf:21324 §Trump Card, DavidK5 v9.0]
row: M149
- Cecilia is a Crest Sorceress; her spells are inscribed onto empty Crest Graphs at magic guilds, split into Black attack and White support magic [wiki:wildarms.fandom.com/Crest Sorcery] [gf:21324 §Adlehyde, DavidK5 v9.0]
- Crests cannot be bought, only found in dungeons, and they are rare [wiki:wildarms.fandom.com/Crest Sorcery] [gf:21324 §A Girl in an Abbey, DavidK5 v9.0]
- In the original game there are 64 spells, half of them advanced and available only at the Isolated Guild, and the supply of Crests is limited, so Cecilia cannot learn them all [wiki:wildarms.fandom.com/Crest Sorcery]
- The spells sit on four 4x4 charts — Basic White, Basic Black, Advanced White, Advanced Black — each indexed on both axes by Geo, Fray, Wing and Muse [gf:21324 §Trump Card, DavidK5 v9.0]
- Crest Graphs turn up in chests and behind puzzles: two at Adlehyde behind a password and a sealed door, a first one from a teacher at Curan Abbey [gf:21324 §Adlehyde, DavidK5 v9.0] [gf:21324 §A Girl in an Abbey, DavidK5 v9.0]

### M150 Fast Draw (Jack's Sword Techniques)
pointers: [wiki:wildarms.fandom.com/Fast Draw] [gf:21324 §Arch Smash, DavidK5 v9.0]
row: M150
- Fast Draw is Jack's set of melee techniques; the wiki lists seventeen for this game [wiki:wildarms.fandom.com/Fast Draw]
- Each one has a hint location rather than a level or a price; DavidK5 tables one for sixteen of them, the first being simply "Start" [gf:21324 §Arch Smash, DavidK5 v9.0]
- Worked locations: a gust of wind on the Mountain Pass gives Sonic Buster; watching boulders fall on Mount Zenom gives Meteor Dive; lighting two torches by a monument in the Sand River gives Heal Blade [gf:21324 §Mountain Pass, DavidK5 v9.0] [gf:21324 §Mount Zenom, DavidK5 v9.0] [gf:21324 §Sand River, DavidK5 v9.0]
- Accepting a warrior statue's challenge at the Tripillar and winning three fights gives Divide Shot; a swordsman in Adlehyde trades Soul Breaker; three machines exploding in the Epitaph Sea give Blast Charge [gf:21324 §Tripillar, DavidK5 v9.0] [gf:21324 §Exploring the Inner Sea, DavidK5 v9.0] [gf:21324 §Epitaph Sea, DavidK5 v9.0]

## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Hidden
- A mirror behind the Duke's spectator area shatters under Rudy's Power Glove, opening a path to the optional boss Angol Moa [gf:21324 §Angol Moa, DavidK5 v9.0]
- The arena's entrance hall and inn hold a Duplicator, a Full Revive and a Power Apple in barrels, and three Crest Graphs sit behind a Duplicator door [gf:21324 §Spell name- Description MP cost, DavidK5 v9.0]

### Upgrades
- An Arms meister upgrades Rudy's guns for a fee, and most towns have one [gf:21324 §Adlehyde, DavidK5 v9.0]

### Shops & exchange
- The Duplicator the fourth round pays is the same key item that opens sealed doors and portals all over the world [gf:21324 §Spell name- Description MP cost, DavidK5 v9.0] [gf:21324 §Epitaph Sea, DavidK5 v9.0]

## Unverified or contradicted

- **Cecilia's "Rod".** M148 named her tools as a Rod and a Pocket Watch. Her four are the Tear Drop, Pocket Watch, Wand and Vase; there is no Rod in this game's list [gf:21324 §The Final Confrontation, DavidK5 v9.0]
- **Who the Divine Blade belongs to.** g064 gave it to Jack. DavidK5 calls it Rudy's best weapon; the wiki names the prize without an owner. The row now names the weapon and not its owner [gf:21324 §Boomerang Flash, DavidK5 v9.0] [wiki:wildarms.fandom.com/Ancient Arena]
- **Seventeen Fast Draws.** The wiki lists seventeen techniques; DavidK5's list carries a hint location for sixteen. The row gives no count [wiki:wildarms.fandom.com/Fast Draw] [gf:21324 §Arch Smash, DavidK5 v9.0]
- **Tools sending you back over old ground.** M148's note already flagged this as the game's weak spot. What both sources do support is narrower: the Radar detects items, Gella and locations on the world map, which is the retroactive part [wiki:wildarms.fandom.com/Radar] [gf:21324 §The Final Confrontation, DavidK5 v9.0]
