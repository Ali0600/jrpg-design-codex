## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Hidden
- With flight and diving, the whole Continent is open, though many overworld encounters are far too strong at that point [gf:82352 §The World (Part 1), chris-williams v1.01]

### Upgrades
- A Picto's level sets how much stat it gives, and the maximum is 33, reached for every Picto only on the fourth playthrough [wiki:clair-obscur.fandom.com/Pictos] [gf:82352 §New Game+, chris-williams v1.01]
- A duplicate of a Picto raises its level and stats [web:game8.co/games/Clair-Obscur-Expedition-33/archives/514371]
- In New Game+, underwater swimming is available from the moment Esquie can swim at all [gf:82352 §New Game+, chris-williams v1.01]

### Shops & exchange

## Unverified or contradicted

- **The expedition counter.** M161 said a counter taken by the whole party is an "expedition counter", when an enemy attacks everyone at once. Only the wiki says so; Game8, chris-williams and CNET describe the counter without it. Cut from the row [wiki:clair-obscur.fandom.com/Battles]
- **The flight rock's name.** The wiki calls it Soarrie and says it was Esquie's first rock. Game8 and chris-williams describe the flight without naming a rock, so the row leaves it unnamed [wiki:clair-obscur.fandom.com/Esquie]
- **Which button counters a Gradient Attack.** Game8 says the Gradient Counter uses the dash input rather than the parry input; no other source read names the button [web:game8.co/games/Clair-Obscur-Expedition-33/archives/515694]
- **Automatic QTE covers attacks only.** CNET says the setting performs the party's own inputs but never a parry or a dodge; the wiki says only that QTEs can be disabled. The row claims the setting, not its limit [web:tech.yahoo.com/gaming/articles/9-tips-starting-clair-obscur-090007233.html]
- **What a skill input is worth.** chris-williams says a skill has one to three inputs and each one landed adds 20% damage, and that a missed input can do worse than lose damage: Gustave's Strike Storm wastes the turn and its AP, Lune's Wildfire kills her. No second source gives a figure [gf:82352 §Combat Tips, chris-williams v1.01]
- **The relationship level Urrie needs.** Game8 says diving requires relationship level 6 with Esquie. chris-williams has Esquie ask for Urrie on a second visit at level 5, with the rematch raising him to 6; the wiki says Urrie is found while reaching level 6. The row says only that it comes with the relationship [web:game8.co/games/Clair-Obscur-Expedition-33/archives/513926] [gf:82352 §Camp (Lumière), chris-williams v1.01] [wiki:clair-obscur.fandom.com/Esquie]
- **The price of Colour of Lumina.** The wiki's item box gives a buy price of 5,000; Game8's merchant list and chris-williams's shop tables give 1,000 Chroma [wiki:clair-obscur.fandom.com/Colour of Lumina] [web:game8.co/games/Clair-Obscur-Expedition-33/archives/515778] [gf:82352 §Merchant - Noco, chris-williams v1.01]
- **Pictos for stats, late on.** chris-williams says a high-level Picto's stat bonuses outgrow what attribute points give (the Anti Stun Picto's 2,757 health against 1,389 from 99 Vitality), so late in the game Pictos are worn for their stats rather than their passives. One source [gf:82352 §Gear Tips, chris-williams v1.01]
