### Legendary Armour (a traversal set found disguised as junk)
cat: Traversal
how: Each piece of the Legendary Armour gives Musashi a new ability the moment he has it: the L-Belt a double jump, the L-Brace a climb up rock walls with both swords, the L-Shoes a grip on icy ledges that are closed to him until they are found, the L-Vest a gauge that charges faster, and the L-Goggles the power to appraise items himself. Most pieces do not look legendary when they turn up — an Old Shirt, a pair of Red Shoes, an Old Cloth, a bracelet in a chest — and only become armour once the pawn shop appraises them. The Old Cloth then offers the set's one choice: woven into either the L-Gloves or the L-Quilt, never both.
loop: Find something that looks like junk → have it appraised → it turns out to be a piece of the legend, and a new way to move → the ledges and walls you already passed open up.
notes: The appraisal step is the idea to steal: the reward hides in plain sight as rubbish, so the player learns to carry every scrap to the appraiser, and one piece — the goggles — removes the need for him, which is itself an upgrade to how the rest are found. Every other piece is a movement verb, so the set doubles as the game's key ring. The one exclusive choice at the end, a critical-hit glove or a sleep-saving quilt, is a light touch worth copying for a set that is otherwise fixed.
verbs: Tool-gated
pointers: [wiki:bravefencermusashi.fandom.com/Legendary Armor] [gf:3718 §5.XI)Legendary Armour, YSF v13.0] [gf:6392 §Unappraised Appraised Value, Nemesis]

## Unverified or contradicted

- **Stolen moves added to a moveset.** M119 said each technique is added to his moveset and a bestiary tracks them. Both walkthroughs say he holds one at a time, and neither ties the bestiary to techniques, so the row is corrected [gf:3718 §5.III)FUSION ABILITY, YSF v13.0] [gf:6392 §ASSIMILATION, Nemesis]
- **What rescued citizens reopen.** M120 said each rescue reopens shops, a restaurant, a church with save perks, minigames and better inventory. YSF's list gives hints, passage, techniques, help in a fight and music; the village's shops belong to villagers the wiki lists by opening hours. The row, loop and notes are rewritten, and the `docs/verbs.md` Consequence quote is re-taken [gf:3718 §5 Remaining Palace members, YSF v13.0] [wiki:bravefencermusashi.fandom.com/Grillin' Village]
- **Events at set hours and weaker fighting.** Nemesis says some areas depend on the clock and a tired Musashi fights worse; YSF says he may fall asleep. Neither detail has a second source, so M121 keeps only that he tires and must sleep [gf:6392 §24-HOUR GAME CLOCK, Nemesis] [gf:3718 §5.V)Sleep, YSF v13.0]

- **Three pieces required to finish.** The wiki says the L-Brace, L-Belt and L-Shoes must be found to finish the game; neither guide says so outright, so the row does not [wiki:bravefencermusashi.fandom.com/Legendary Armor]
- **How many pieces the legend counts.** YSF opens with the legend's five pieces, then counts seven with both of the L-Cloth's products, or six since only one can be made; the wiki lists six entries with the cloth's two outcomes under one. The row names the pieces rather than a count [gf:3718 §5.XI)Legendary Armour, YSF v13.0] [wiki:bravefencermusashi.fandom.com/Legendary Armor]
