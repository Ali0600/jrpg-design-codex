## Unverified or contradicted

- **The Strike Area's +20%.** M163 said a Strike Area adds 20% damage and that criticals stack more. The wiki gives +20% and +50% for a critical; sophialeigh and threetimes say only that it does more damage. Cut from the row [wiki:shadowhearts.fandom.com/Judgement Ring]
- **The ring outside battle.** M163 said the ring gates field actions such as opening doors. The wiki says a Ring Trial sometimes opens a door or turns a valve; neither guide read mentions one. Cut from the row, which keeps the shops [wiki:shadowhearts.fandom.com/Judgement Ring]
- **Affinity's +20%.** M165 said Affinity is worth up to +20% per follow-up. The wiki gives 0.2% per point to a maximum of 100, and 2% per hit already landed; both guides confirm the effects but give no figures. Cut from the row [wiki:shadowhearts.fandom.com/Combo System] [wiki:shadowhearts.fandom.com/Affinity]
- **The mathematics exam.** g069 listed a fourth quiz, a mathematics exam in Cannes that pays no item but points the party to Lenny. Only the wiki has it; neither guide read mentions it. Cut from the row and its table [wiki:shadowhearts.fandom.com/Quiz/Shadow Hearts: Covenant]
- **A wrong quiz answer.** The wiki says a wrong answer starts the quiz over with no penalty; sophialeigh says only that all ten must be right [wiki:shadowhearts.fandom.com/Quiz/Shadow Hearts: Covenant] [gf:49713 §Quizzes (sqz), sophialeigh v1.25]
- **No HP or MP restored between trials.** Only the wiki says so [wiki:shadowhearts.fandom.com/Pit Fights/Shadow Hearts: Covenant]
- **Solomon's Key's 72 crests.** The wiki says the Solomon Crest needs all seventy-two crests found and placed; sophialeigh says only that the key is filled in. The row no longer gives the number [wiki:shadowhearts.fandom.com/Solomon Crest] [gf:49713 §Creator, sophialeigh v1.25]
- **Who trades the Nemesis Fan.** The wiki names Malkovich, an Ordinary Citizen; sophialeigh names Margarete, the babysitter from the first game. The row says only that the trader is in the cathedral district [wiki:shadowhearts.fandom.com/Straw Millionaire Trading Quest] [gf:49713 §Item Trading (sit), sophialeigh v1.25]
- **The Dating Outfit.** The wiki says taking the cash locks the player out of the Dating Outfit; sophialeigh says buying the fan later still sends Margarete to Roger, so the outfit can be had either way [wiki:shadowhearts.fandom.com/Straw Millionaire Trading Quest] [gf:49713 §Item Trading (sit), sophialeigh v1.25]
- **When the straw appears.** The wiki says the quest opens once the rain stops in Southampton; sophialeigh does not mention rain [wiki:shadowhearts.fandom.com/Straw Millionaire Trading Quest]
