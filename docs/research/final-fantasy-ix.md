### M086 Ability learning from equipment
pointers: [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Final Fantasy IX abilities] [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Magic Stone (Final Fantasy IX)] [gf:29957 §2.3 - Ability System, Andrew_Testa v96]
row: M086
- Equipped gear lets a character use the abilities in it; winning battles adds AP to each, and a mastered ability stays usable after the item comes off [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Final Fantasy IX abilities] [gf:29957 §2.3 - Ability System, Andrew_Testa v96]
- AP already earned is kept when the item is unequipped, so an ability can be finished later [gf:29957 §2.3 - Ability System, Andrew_Testa v96]
- The AP an ability needs differs from character to character [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Final Fantasy IX abilities] [gf:29957 §2.3 - Ability System, Andrew_Testa v96]
- Support abilities work only with Magic Stones assigned, a stronger ability costing more, and the stone total rises with level [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Magic Stone (Final Fantasy IX)] [gf:29957 §2.3 - Ability System, Andrew_Testa v96]
