### M145 Tactical Arts System (Arts Discovery)
pointers: [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Tactical Arts System] [gf:4736 §Source: Legend of Legaia, Dan_GC v0.8]
row: M145
- An attack is composed from directions instead of a button: Right and Left strike with that arm, High and Low at that height, and each has its own uses against a given enemy [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Tactical Arts System]
- Certain strings trigger Arts, which do far more damage than the same number of loose hits [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Tactical Arts System] [gf:4736 §Source: Legend of Legaia, Dan_GC v0.8]
- Arts are uncovered by experimenting or revealed by NPCs, and an Art performed without learning it from someone is recorded in the Arts list [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Tactical Arts System] [gf:4736 §Source: Legend of Legaia, Dan_GC v0.8]
- Arts cost AP, earned three ways: dealing physical damage without an Art, taking damage, or spending a turn on the Spirit command [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Tactical Arts System] [gf:4736 §Source: Legend of Legaia, Dan_GC v0.8]
- Entering an Art's inputs without the AP just performs the loose string, with no animation or bonus [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Tactical Arts System]
- Super Arts are chained combinations of normal Arts and are the one kind the game never records in the Arts menu [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Tactical Arts System] [gf:4736 §Source: Legend of Legaia, Dan_GC v0.8]
- Hyper Arts are learned only from elemental books, three per character, one from a chest and the rest held by bosses [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Tactical Arts System] [gf:4736 §Source: Legend of Legaia, Dan_GC v0.8] [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Seru]
- Per character: 11 Arts, five Super Arts, three Hyper Arts and one Miracle Art, and every Miracle Art costs 99 AP [gf:4736 §Source: Legend of Legaia, Dan_GC v0.8] [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Tactical Arts System]

### M146 Ra-Seru & Seru Absorption
pointers: [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Seru] [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Ra-Seru] [gf:4736 §5) Miracle Arts, Dan_GC v0.8]
row: M146
- A Ra-Seru bonds to its partner and strengthens their primary arm for combat [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Seru] [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Ra-Seru]
- Defeating a Seru lets the Ra-Seru absorb its power for later use, and calling on that power repeatedly levels it up, making it stronger and adding effects [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Seru]
- Absorbing is not automatic: how the Seru is killed matters, and killing it with too much damage or launching it lowers the chance [gf:4736 §5) Miracle Arts, Dan_GC v0.8]
- Each character has a best Art and Super Art for absorbing — Vahn's Power Punch and Maximum Blow, Noa's Tough Love and Love You, Gala's Black Rain and Heaven's Drop [gf:4736 §5) Miracle Arts, Dan_GC v0.8]
- Seru and Ra-Seru magic level to nine at most [gf:4736 §5) Miracle Arts, Dan_GC v0.8]
- The Ra-Seru visibly grows each time a Genesis Tree is activated, and its element decides the Hyper Arts it can learn [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Seru] [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Ra-Seru]

### M147 Genesis Trees & Mist De-fogging
pointers: [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Genesis Tree] [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Seru]
row: M147
- Genesis Trees are scattered across Legaia and all of them have withered [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Genesis Tree]
- A revived tree drives the Mist from the surrounding area, keeps Seru at bay and turns Seru monsters back into humans [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Genesis Tree] [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Seru]
- Mist makes a Seru attack humans, and a Seru already bound to a human takes the host over, so removing the Mist frees the person unharmed and un-aged [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Seru]
- A withered tree left too long in the Mist dies for good [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Genesis Tree]
- The trees are the Ra-Seru's homes, and activating one is what makes the partner Ra-Seru grow [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Genesis Tree] [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Seru]

## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Hidden
- Arts are also taught by NPCs met around the world, which makes townsfolk worth talking to twice [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Tactical Arts System] [gf:4736 §Source: Legend of Legaia, Dan_GC v0.8]

### Upgrades
- Arts can be linked when one ends on the input another begins with, and a longer overlap links more cheaply; a Hyper Art can start a link but cannot be linked into [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Tactical Arts System]

### Shops & exchange
- Sol's fishing exchange at Vidna sells the War God Icon for 50,000 points, the Mettle Armband for 1,500 and the Power Ring for 1,000 [gf:53721 §Total: 186/260 71.53%, meth962 v1.11]

## Unverified or contradicted

- **What the Speed Chain does.** Dan_GC says it gives its wearer the first turn in battle; A_I_e_x's item table says it gives the wearer the last turn. g063 no longer says which [gf:4736 §5. Speed Chain, Dan_GC v0.8] [gf:37808 §Soru Bread | Restores Max HP to single, A_I_e_x]
- **The dance score that pays it.** Dan_GC puts the threshold at over 500 points in the contest; meth962 says over 400 in the King's lessons, or simply winning the contest. The row says a high enough score [gf:4736 §5. Speed Chain, Dan_GC v0.8] [gf:53721 §Total: 181/260 69.61%, meth962 v1.11]
- **What Expert and Master strip.** Dan_GC: Expert bars armour, Master bars armour and magic, with weapons explicitly still allowed. meth962: on Master neither armour nor weapon is allowed, though accessories and the Ra-Seru stay. Both agree the courses strip gear and that accessories remain, which is what the row now says [gf:4736 §5. Warman (7 entries), Dan_GC v0.8] [gf:53721 §Secrets: Muscle Dome, meth962 v1.11]
- **The Expert and Master coin payouts.** g062 gave about 1,532 and about 13,856 Coins. No source read here carries either; only the Beginner payout appears, as 818 in 37808's walkthrough body and "800 some tokens" in meth962. The other two are cut [gf:53721 §Total: 181/260 69.61%, meth962 v1.11]
- **Jette as a superboss, and the Icon lost forever.** Jette's fortress is a story dungeon in both walkthroughs, not an optional superboss, and meth962 reports the early-Master problem as what many people claim rather than as a rule; he also buys the same accessory at Vidna's fishing exchange later. The row says the Master prize comes after Jette's fortress and that clearing it early is reported to forfeit it [gf:53721 §Total: 186/260 71.53%, meth962 v1.11] [gf:4736 §7. War God Icon, Dan_GC v0.8]
- **An "Art block count that grows as characters level".** M145 said so. Neither the wiki's system page nor Dan_GC describes a per-turn input allowance that grows; the row drops it [wiki:legaia.fandom.com/Tactical Arts System] [gf:4736 §Source: Legend of Legaia, Dan_GC v0.8]
