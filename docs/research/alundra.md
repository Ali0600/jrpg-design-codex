### M151 Dreamwalking (Dream-Dive Dungeons)
pointers: [wiki:alundra.fandom.com/Alundra] [gf:3691 §OOOOOOOOO OOOOOOOOO O O O O O OOOOOOOOO, HLaPierre v2]
row: M151
- Alundra is an elf of the clan of Elna, the Dreamwalkers, and in Inoa he enters the villagers' dreams to rid them of the nightmares possessing them [wiki:alundra.fandom.com/Alundra]
- Each dive is its own dungeon belonging to one named villager: Wendell's, Olen's, Bonaire's, Kline's [gf:3691 §OOOOOOOOO OOOOOOOOO O O O O O OOOOOOOOO, HLaPierre v2] [wiki:alundra.fandom.com/Alundra]
- Clearing the nightmare is what saves the sleeper: Alundra destroys the slime at the core of Wendell's dream and Wendell wakes in good health [wiki:alundra.fandom.com/Alundra]
- It does not always work. Olen takes a fatal wound inside his own dream, Alundra is pushed out of his mind, and Olen dies in front of the party moments later [gf:3691 §OOOOOOOOO OOOOOOOOO O O O O O OOOOOOOOO, HLaPierre v2] [wiki:alundra.fandom.com/Alundra]
- Villagers keep being murdered in their dreams as the game goes on, and some of them start blaming Alundra for it [wiki:alundra.fandom.com/Alundra]

### M152 Gilded Falcons & Life Vessels (One Per Dungeon)
pointers: [wiki:alundra.fandom.com/Gilded Falcon] [wiki:alundra.fandom.com/Life Vessel] [gf:3691 §OOOOOOOOO OOOOOOOOO O O O O O OOOOOOOOO, HLaPierre v2]
row: M152
- There are 50 Gilded Falcons, typically one per dungeon, and some are permanently missable because of that [wiki:alundra.fandom.com/Gilded Falcon]
- There are 40 Life Vessels, each adding one to maximum HP for a total of 50, again typically one per dungeon and again some missable [wiki:alundra.fandom.com/Life Vessel]
- Falcons are handed to Captain Merrick for unique items [wiki:alundra.fandom.com/Gilded Falcon] [gf:3691 §OOOOOOOOO OOOOOOOOO O O O O O OOOOOOOOO, HLaPierre v2]
- The ladder: Life Vessel at 15, Olga's Ring at 20, Life Vessel at 25, Silver Armlet at 30, Life Vessel at 35, Recovery Ring at 40, Life Vessel at 45 [gf:3691 §OOOOOOOOO OOOOOOOOO O O O O O OOOOOOOOO, HLaPierre v2] [wiki:alundra.fandom.com/Life Vessel]
- All 50 Falcons buy the Spirit Wand, which does no damage but makes magic energy and prevents direct attacks [gf:38538 §The Village of Inoa, PhamtonPain v1.1]
- Merrick's shop opens after the Ancient Shrine, and the exchange itself only starts once a sailor in the pub mentions he is looking for Falcons [gf:38538 §The Village of Inoa, PhamtonPain v1.1] [gf:3691 §OOOOOOOOO OOOOOOOOO O O O O O OOOOOOOOO, HLaPierre v2]
- Two Life Vessels are bought rather than found: 500 gilder at the Inoa shop, 400 at Lurvy's smithy after buying the Leather Armour [wiki:alundra.fandom.com/Life Vessel]

### M153 Puzzle-Box Dungeons (Platforming + Logic as Content)
pointers: [wiki:alundra.fandom.com/Alundra] [gf:3691 §OOOOOOOOO OOOOOOOOO O O O O O OOOOOOOOO, HLaPierre v2]
row: M153
- The game mixes top-down platforming with action-adventure and RPG parts, close to the Zelda shape [wiki:alundra.fandom.com/Alundra]
- It is known for extremely difficult puzzles, and for puzzles that can no longer be reached once the player has moved on, which makes some items unattainable [wiki:alundra.fandom.com/Alundra]
- Terrain itself is part of the puzzle: sand slows you, lava burns you, and upgrades are what let you get past such obstacles, which is the game's own encouragement to explore [wiki:alundra.fandom.com/Alundra]
- Rooms are logic boxes of levers, gates, timed doors, thrown boxes and precise jumps, and their solutions are the gate on progress [gf:3691 §OOOOOOOOO OOOOOOOOO O O O O O OOOOOOOOO, HLaPierre v2] [gf:38538 §The Village of Inoa, PhamtonPain v1.1]

## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Hidden
- A Gilded Falcon can be lost to timing rather than to a locked door: Olen's nightmare only registers the chest if its animation finishes before Septimus calls Alundra out, and Olen's house is destroyed shortly after the Coal Mine [wiki:alundra.fandom.com/Gilded Falcon]

### Upgrades
- Two Life Vessels come from clearing dungeons outright, at the Coal Mine and Lars' Crypt, and the Crypt's is not missable because its exit only appears once all three reward chests are opened [wiki:alundra.fandom.com/Life Vessel]

### Shops & exchange
- Merrick prices everything at 10,000 gilder and Alundra can only carry 9,999, so the only way to buy is with Falcons [gf:38538 §The Village of Inoa, PhamtonPain v1.1] [gf:3691 §OOOOOOOOO OOOOOOOOO O O O O O OOOOOOOOO, HLaPierre v2]

## Unverified or contradicted

- **The twins' blue and red halves.** M151 described a timid twin's dungeon rendered in cautious blues and his brash brother's in fevered reds. Neither guide nor the wiki describes any such pairing, so the row now says the dungeon is shaped to its sleeper without inventing an example [wiki:alundra.fandom.com/Alundra] [gf:3691 §OOOOOOOOO OOOOOOOOO O O O O O OOOOOOOOO, HLaPierre v2]
- **"Demonstrate, then invert".** M153 said a recurring pattern demonstrates a solution in one room and demands its mirror or inversion in another. No source read here describes that structure; what both describe is difficulty, precision platforming and puzzles that gate progress. The claim is cut, and with it the row's *Inscription deduction* verb tag, whose quoted evidence was that sentence [wiki:alundra.fandom.com/Alundra] [gf:3691 §OOOOOOOOO OOOOOOOOO O O O O O OOOOOOOOO, HLaPierre v2]
- **One Vessel and one Falcon in "nearly every" dungeon.** The wiki says "typically" one of each per dungeon, and its own lists include vessels bought in shops and falcons sitting in houses and on ledges. The row keeps the pattern and drops the "nearly every" [wiki:alundra.fandom.com/Life Vessel] [wiki:alundra.fandom.com/Gilded Falcon]
