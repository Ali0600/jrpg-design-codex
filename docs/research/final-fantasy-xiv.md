## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Hidden
- The Fenrir mount costs 1,000,000 MGP [wiki:ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/Fenrir (Mount)]
- MGP also buys Setzer's costume, a bunny outfit and NPC casual wear, and since patch 7.1 copies of Maxims of Mahjong at 100,000 MGP each [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/The Gold Saucer (Final Fantasy XIV)]
- Each tournament has its own rare card: Spinner's Pull pays Cecil Harvey, Rowena's Cup Classic Tidus, Orran Durai Memorial Firion and the Manderville Tournament of Champions Lightning [wiki:ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/Triple Triad]

### Upgrades
- Tournament rating adds Platinum Triad Card packs: four at 1,800 or more, three at 1,600–1,799, two at 1,100–1,599 [wiki:ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/Triple Triad]
- Higher-rated races pay far more race EXP and slightly more MGP, every finish but last returns more MGP than the entry fee, and one of 2nd to 7th place wins a roulette of bonus EXP [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Chocobo Racing (Final Fantasy XIV)] [wiki:ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/Chocobo Racing]

### Shops & exchange
- Duplicate cards sell for MGP by rarity, from 100 for one star to 1,500 for five [wiki:ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/Triple Triad]
- Grade 3 racing feeds cost 1,345 MGP each at the Tack & Feed Trader [wiki:ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/Chocobo Racing]
- Bronze Triad card packs cost 520 MGP and Silver packs 1,152 [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Triple Triad (Final Fantasy XIV)]

## Unverified or contradicted
- **Platinum packs by placement.** The Final Fantasy Wiki puts a Platinum Triad Card in the 4th to 10th place rewards; the Console Games Wiki gives Platinum packs by tournament rating instead [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Triple Triad (Final Fantasy XIV)] [wiki:ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/Triple Triad]
- **The top three's MGP.** The Console Games Wiki gives 140,000 MGP; the Final Fantasy Wiki says only that MGP scales with rank [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Triple Triad (Final Fantasy XIV)] [wiki:ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/Triple Triad]
