## Mechanics candidates

No new rows. The blocks below source the three existing rows, each edited in place on 2026-09-17.

### M106 Weapon affinity growth (weapons with memory)
pointers: [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Vagrant Story] [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Vagrant Story weapons] [gf:7745 §ADVANCED WEAPON DEDICATION, ZNicholson v1.3]
row: M106
- Weapons gain the class affinities of the enemies they are used against, and armor gains the affinities of the attacks it takes [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Vagrant Story] [gf:7745 §ADVANCED WEAPON DEDICATION, ZNicholson v1.3]
- There are six creature classes (Human, Beast, Undead, Phantom, Dragon, Evil) and elemental affinities as well [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Vagrant Story] [gf:7745 §ADVANCED WEAPON DEDICATION, ZNicholson v1.3]
- A gain in one affinity often comes with a loss in another, so no weapon is equally strong against every foe [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Vagrant Story] [gf:7745 §ADVANCED WEAPON DEDICATION, ZNicholson v1.3]

### M107 Workshop crafting (assemble, combine, break down)
pointers: [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Vagrant Story] [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Vagrant Story weapons] [gf:7745 §12. THE WORKSHOP, ZNicholson v1.3] [gf:7745 §13. COMBINATION, FORGING AND REFORGING, ZNicholson v1.3]
row: M107
- Workshops are set places, each with tools for certain materials, and any of them can repair equipment [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Vagrant Story] [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Vagrant Story weapons] [gf:7745 §12. THE WORKSHOP, ZNicholson v1.3]
- Weapons are assembled from a blade and a grip, can be broken down and put back together differently, and take gems [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Vagrant Story] [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Vagrant Story weapons] [gf:7745 §ADVANCED WEAPON DEDICATION, ZNicholson v1.3]
- Combining two items makes a new one that keeps what the old ones had gained, and combination usually works in the player's favor [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Vagrant Story] [gf:7745 §13. COMBINATION, FORGING AND REFORGING, ZNicholson v1.3]

### M108 Chain abilities + Risk (push-your-luck combat)
pointers: [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Chain Abilities] [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Risk] [gf:7745 §7. RISK, ZNicholson v1.3]
row: M108
- Pressing a battle ability as the exclamation mark appears chains another attack, or triggers a defense ability when being hit [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Chain Abilities] [gf:7745 §7. RISK, ZNicholson v1.3]
- Chaining raises Risk, which lowers hit and evasion and raises the damage taken [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Risk] [gf:7745 §7. RISK, ZNicholson v1.3]

## Unverified or contradicted

- **What the weapon learns from.** M106 said a weapon learns from what it KILLS. The wiki says the enemies it is used against, and the walkthrough says each first hit, so the row now says what it hits [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Vagrant Story] [gf:7745 §ADVANCED WEAPON DEDICATION, ZNicholson v1.3]
- **Risk and damage dealt.** The wiki says higher Risk raises critical hits; the walkthrough says it lowers damage inflicted. The row names only what both say, lower accuracy and more damage taken [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Risk] [gf:7745 §7. RISK, ZNicholson v1.3]
- **Chains without end, gems in battle, Bronze and Iron making Hagane.** "Indefinitely" is in neither source (the walkthrough says Risk exists so chains cannot run on); gems swapped even in battle is on the wiki alone; the metal recipes are in the walkthrough alone. All are cut from the rows [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Vagrant Story] [gf:7745 §13. COMBINATION, FORGING AND REFORGING, ZNicholson v1.3]
