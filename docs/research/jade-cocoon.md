### M157 Minion Capture (Cocoon Master)
pointers: [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Cocoon] [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Minions] [gf:6364 §KORIS THE BLUE COCOON MASTER, Stinger_316 v2.0] [gf:5070 §II. The basics, MindWandererB v0.99]
row: M157
- Levant receives the Flute of Capture from Mother Garai, and capturing is taught as: attack the minion first, then try to capture it [gf:6364 §KORIS THE BLUE COCOON MASTER, Stinger_316 v2.0] [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Minions]
- A capture traps the minion in an empty cocoon; Mahbu or Ra refill the empties to a maximum of 12, and an attempt spends one whether it works or not [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Cocoon]
- The catch comes back as a firefly cocoon, unusable until a Nagi woman purifies it [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Cocoon] [gf:6364 §2. MAHBU, Stinger_316 v2.0] [gf:6364 §KORIS THE BLUE COCOON MASTER, Stinger_316 v2.0]
- Minions appear as enemies only in the forests, and a purified one is summoned in battle with the same flute [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Minions]
- Higher-level minions are harder to catch, and it gets easier as Levant's capture level rises, which happens by capturing successfully [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Minions] [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Levant]
- A minion can be spun into silk and sold for weapons and armour, and the higher its level the more the silk is worth — though only 20 of any one item can be carried, so silk spun past that is lost [gf:5070 §II. The basics, MindWandererB v0.99]
- Levant himself gains no experience from killing minions [gf:5070 §II. The basics, MindWandererB v0.99]

### M158 Minion Merging (Fusion)
pointers: [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Merging] [gf:10553 §An Overview, Goryus v2.5] [gf:10553 §Appearance, Goryus v2.5]
row: M158
- Merging is only available after speaking to Mahbu or Ra, and it fuses two captured minions into one [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Merging] [gf:10553 §What is Merging?, Goryus v2.5]
- The first minion chosen is the base: its stats are what rise or fall, and its posture and method of attack carry over whatever it is fused to [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Merging] [gf:10553 §An Overview, Goryus v2.5]
- The second has only minor effect — it changes the base's skin and a third of its body, its own stats are ignored, and it moves the result's growth rates [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Merging]
- A merge result can be merged again, five or six deep, and appearance is tracked in 50 slots: a merge overwrites 25 of them, so the creature drifts further from its original form with each one [gf:10553 §Appearance, Goryus v2.5] [gf:10553 §What is Merging?, Goryus v2.5]
- Elements combine and colour the skin — fire red, water blue, earth green, wind yellow, so a fire fused to a water comes out purple [gf:10553 §An Overview, Goryus v2.5]
- The higher a minion's share of an element, the more damage its spells of that element do, and with no share of an element its spells of that element always do 0 [gf:10553 §An Overview, Goryus v2.5]
- Growth is a dot on a four-way grid (Attack, Defense, Speed, Magic) whose position also sets HP and MP, and opposite corners cannot both be high, so a build is a real choice [gf:10553 §Stats, Goryus v2.5]
- Fusing a stronger minion onto a weaker base is the mistake to avoid: the result takes the stronger one's level but the average of the two's stats [gf:10553 §An Overview, Goryus v2.5]

### M159 The Eternal Corridor (Endless Post-Game Dungeon)
pointers: [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Eternal Corridor] [gf:10553 §An Overview, Goryus v2.5]
row: M159
- After the story ends the player saves, reloads, and is sent through the Kumari Gate into the Eternal Corridor; the game's ordinary forests close [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Eternal Corridor]
- It is 1,000 stages deep, and leaving stage 1,000 puts you back in 999 [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Eternal Corridor]
- A stage has one entrance and one exit, up to seven dead ends, and no way back to an earlier stage [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Eternal Corridor]
- At the exit one to three special mini-bosses from a set of 8 attack [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Eternal Corridor]
- Being defeated resets the whole dungeon to stage 1 [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Eternal Corridor]
- 136 obtainable minions live here, up to three types per stage, some of them from the main game and some only here [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Eternal Corridor]
- A stage's colour tells you the element inside: a red forest holds only fire minions, and a colourless one can hold any [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Eternal Corridor]
- Bosses need not be killed — retreating is enough — but killing one can pay a boss Skin, which replaces a minion's skin during a merge [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Eternal Corridor] [gf:10553 §Appearance, Goryus v2.5]

## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Hidden
- Only one minion, Bauback, can appear in a forest whose colour does not match its element [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Eternal Corridor]

### Upgrades
- Minions in the Corridor start at level 20 and climb by one every four stages, to level 26 [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Eternal Corridor]

### Shops & exchange
- Silk is the economy: minions spun into silk are what pay for better weapons and armour [gf:5070 §II. The basics, MindWandererB v0.99]

## Unverified or contradicted

- **A level 54 cap that only merging passes.** M158's note said merging is the only route past level 54, so the top tier is locked behind fusion. No source read here mentions a level cap at all; Goryus's guide, which is entirely about merging, never raises one. The claim is cut [gf:10553 §An Overview, Goryus v2.5] [gf:10553 §Stats, Goryus v2.5]
- **The capture level.** That capturing successfully raises Levant's capture level, and that this is what makes harder minions catchable, is stated by the Jade Cocoon Wiki on two pages; none of the three guides read discusses it. The row keeps the claim — it is the evidence for its *Mastery reveal* verb tag — but it rests on that one wiki [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Minions] [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Levant]
- **"Every forest monster is acquirable."** M157's note said so. The sources say minions appear as enemies only in the forests and that higher-level ones are harder to catch; none says every species can be caught. The note now says the forests are the collectible pool without claiming completeness [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Minions]
- **The second parent as purely cosmetic.** It changes the skin and a third of the body and its own stats are ignored, but it does move the result's growth rates, so it is not cosmetic alone — which is what the row already said and the wiki confirms exactly [wiki:jadecocoon.fandom.com/Merging]
