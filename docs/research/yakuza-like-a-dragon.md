## Mechanics candidates

### Part-Time Hero: a city quest board whose ranks pay gear
cat: Side Content & Minigames
how: From chapter 5 Ichiban works for Part-Time Hero, an online service posting three kinds of request around Ijincho: support quests that ask for items handed in at kiosks, rescue quests that are street fights marked on the map, and challenge quests that are the old completion list. Support and rescue quests pay money, items and points, and the points climb through four rank-ups from Junior to Executive Hero, each collected as a piece of gear, from a Parka to a Ceramic Boxcutter. Challenge quests pay in personality stats instead.
loop: take a request from the board → hand in the items or win the street fight → money and points → each rank-up collected as new gear → feels good because helping strangers is also a ladder
notes: This pairs with M228: substories pay allies, and this board pays gear on a visible ladder, so the city's small jobs have two separate payoffs. The shape to copy is the split by quest type: fetch and fight requests pay money and rank points, while completion challenges pay the character's personality stats, so every kind of side activity grows something different. Give each rank-up a named reward handed over in person, as here; a ladder with nothing at its rungs is just a number.

## Minigame candidates

### Can Quest: cans for Eco Points, and a shop of growth items, CDs and casino tokens
p: From the homeless camp, Ichiban pedals a converted bike around the district collecting cans against the clock and must get back to the start. Single cans and piles of 30 sit on the map; rival collectors ram him for 50 cans unless he hits them first with an energy-drink boost, and the garbage truck costs 100. Returning with at least the course's goal (50, 200 or 300 cans) converts the haul into Eco Points at a multiplier set by the rank reached.
r: Eco Points spent at Kan-san's shop, from pocket change and junk up to 100,000-point growth items (manuals and stat boosters), music CDs including three Persona 5 soundtrack discs, and a casino token at 200,000.
l: A score attack whose points buy real growth: the dearest shelf holds manuals and boosters, so a silly minigame feeds the main build, and the rank multiplier makes a good run worth far more than a long one. For the owner's game, price progression items high enough that the venue becomes a place to return to, and let skill (the rank) multiply the payout rather than time.

## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Hidden
- The Elegant Pouch weapon goes on sale at Kan-san's shop, for 500,000 Eco Points, only after 10,000 cans have been collected in total [gf:78735 §Can Quest, CyricZ v1.3]

### Upgrades
- Part-Time Hero's points to each rank: Junior 15, Senior 70, Veteran 150, Executive 300, and the Executive rank also unlocks the "A New Hero" trophy [gf:78191 §Rank System, shockinblue v1.5]
- Can Quest raises the Kindness stat, and clearing all three courses earns the Can Quest Hero trophy [gf:78191 §Can Quest, shockinblue v1.5]
- Can Quest's multipliers by rank: Easy ×2, ×4, ×6 and ×10 at 50, 100, 200 and 300 cans; Medium ×4, ×6, ×8 and ×12 at 200, 300, 400 and 500; Hard ×5, ×8, ×10 and ×15 at 300, 400, 500 and 700 [gf:78191 §Course Select, shockinblue v1.5]
- Every can collected, win or lose, counts toward milestones paying bonus Eco Points: 5,000 at 1,000 cans, 10,000 at 2,500, 25,000 at 5,000 and 100,000 at 10,000 [gf:78735 §Can Quest, CyricZ v1.3]

### Shops & exchange
- The shop's cheap end: 10 yen for 150 points, 100 yen for 1,500, 500 yen for 7,500, and junk and worn gear from 100 to 2,800 [gf:78735 §Can Quest, CyricZ v1.3] [gf:78191 §Exchange Shop, shockinblue v1.5]
- Casino medals sell for 30,000 to 100,000 points [gf:78735 §Can Quest, CyricZ v1.3] [gf:78191 §Exchange Shop, shockinblue v1.5]
- The business minigame runs in periods of four Open for Business sessions and one shareholder meeting; its funds are kept apart from Ichiban's own money, successful meetings pay him executive bonuses, and higher rank allows more properties [gf:78735 §Business Management, CyricZ v1.3]
