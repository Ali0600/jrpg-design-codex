### M169 Alchemy Pot
pointers: [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Alchemy Pot] [gf:41297 §(II-B4) The Alchemy Pot, Red_Scarlet v06] [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Liquid metal sword] [gf:57861 §Legal/Contact info, Granamyr v.75]
row: M169
- King Trode repairs the pot salvaged from his castle and hands it to the Hero [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Alchemy Pot] [gf:41297 §BOSS FIGHT, Red_Scarlet v06]
- At first it takes two ingredients, and after the Pickham incident Trode upgrades it to take three [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Alchemy Pot] [gf:41297 §(II-B4) The Alchemy Pot, Red_Scarlet v06]
- In the PlayStation 2 original a mix finishes only after the party has walked some distance (not in dungeons), announced by a ding; the 3DS remake made it instant [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Alchemy Pot] [gf:41297 §(II-B4) The Alchemy Pot, Red_Scarlet v06]
- Some of the best gear exists only through alchemy: the Liquid Metal Sword needs Orichalcum, a Rusty Old Sword and a Slime Crown [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Liquid metal sword] [gf:57861 §Legal/Contact info, Granamyr v.75]
- Results feed further mixes: a Short Bow and a Chain Whip make a Hunter's Bow, which with a Garter makes an Eros' Bow, which makes a Cheiron's Bow and, with that bow and a Great Bow, Odin's Bow, the strongest bow [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Hunter's bow] [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Eros' bow] [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Odin's bow] [gf:57861 §Legal/Contact info, Granamyr v.75]
- Some recipes are notes hidden around the world, a hide-and-seek game set by Farebury's armour-shop merchant: the Thief's Key recipe by Farebury's church bell at night, the Eros' Bow recipe behind crates by Pickham's watchtower [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Eros' bow] [gf:43062 §FAREBURY, Dunatis2000 v1.2] [gf:43062 §PICKHAM, Dunatis2000 v1.2]

## Exploration & upgrade facts

- The arena's rank list runs G, F, E, D, C, B, A, with S revealed after A; entry costs 100 gold at G rising to 1,000 at A [gf:41297 §(II-F) Revisiting the Past for a Ship, Red_Scarlet v06] [gf:41297 §(II-J) Jessica Takes a Vacation, Red_Scarlet v06]

### Upgrades
- The Hero's Sword skill pays attack bonuses and Metal Slash, Falcon Slash, Miracle Slash and Gigaslash on the way to 100 [gf:44904 §a01. Sword (DQ026), GaranP v0.91]
- Winning Rank E also pays a Ring of Clarity [gf:41297 §(II-F) Revisiting the Past for a Ship, Red_Scarlet v06]

### Shops & exchange
- Casino prizes can be sold for gold; Prayer Rings bought with tokens sell for 1,500 gold each, which the guides use to turn tokens into money [gf:40336 §5.10 Baccarat's Prizes, Okamotosan v0.9] [gf:43062 §CASINOS IN BACCARAT AND PICKHAM, Dunatis2000 v1.2]

## Unverified or contradicted

- **A failed mix.** M169 said a failed mix returns the ingredients. Red_Scarlet says a dud pops its items straight back out; no second source read says so. Cut from the row [gf:41297 §(II-B4) The Alchemy Pot, Red_Scarlet v06]
- **Bookshelves and four notes.** M169 said formulas come from bookshelves and townsfolk and from four barely visible notes. The notes' hide-and-seek game is confirmed, but neither the count nor the bookshelves could be cited from a second source (A_I_e_x mentions a bookshelf recipe under an uncitable heading). The row says formulas turn up around the world [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Eros' bow]
- **Metal Slash's threshold.** M171 said the Hero learns Metal Slash at 30 Sword. The wiki says 30; GaranP's table says 32. The row no longer gives it [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Metal Slash] [gf:44904 §a01. Sword (DQ026), GaranP v0.91]
- **Monsters scaling to the Hero.** M170 said recruited monsters level with the Hero, so none goes obsolete. No source read says so. Cut from the row [gf:41297 §(II-F) Revisiting the Past for a Ship, Red_Scarlet v06]
- **A perk at every rank.** M170 said each rank grants a fixed prize plus a permanent perk. Every rank pays an item, and Rank E alone teaches a technique (Call Team). The row now says so [gf:41297 §(II-F) Revisiting the Past for a Ship, Red_Scarlet v06]
- **Poker.** g073 listed a poker Double-or-Nothing ladder. Neither casino has poker in any source read: the wiki and Okamotosan name slots, roulette and bingo, and A_I_e_x the same. Cut from the row [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Casino] [gf:40336 §3.10 Starting Casino, Okamotosan v0.9]
- **Tokens one way only.** g073 said gold buys tokens but never the reverse. No source read says so; prizes can be sold for gold. Cut from the row [gf:40336 §5.10 Baccarat's Prizes, Okamotosan v0.9]
- **Bingo's debut.** The wiki says bingo and roulette were both introduced in this game; nothing else read says so, and the row no longer claims it [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Casino]
- **Prize stats.** g073's table gave stats (Agility +15, the Falcon Blade striking twice, the Gringham Whip's 127 attack hitting every enemy). The wiki gives them; Okamotosan's lists give only prices and sell prices. The table keeps names and prices [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Casino]
- **Hunter's Bow as an alchemy result.** The wiki gives two recipes for it; Granamyr's table lists it as made from nothing. The wiki is kept, since the bow chain the row's notes describe starts there [wiki:dragonquest.fandom.com/Hunter's bow] [gf:57861 §Legal/Contact info, Granamyr v.75]
