## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Hidden
- Topping the darts board also earns the Heavenly Dart Player trophy [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Darts (VII Remake)] [web:game8.co/games/FF7-Remake/archives/286495]
- The squat and pull-up ladders each end in a trophy, Sultan of Squat and Peeress of Pull-Ups [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Squats (VII Remake)] [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Pull-Ups] [web:game8.co/games/FF7-Remake/archives/286540] [web:game8.co/games/FF7-Remake/archives/286413]
- Whack-a-Box returns in Final Fantasy VII Rebirth [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Whack-a-Box]

### Upgrades
- The Trainee squat match is the Burning Thighs side quest, and the Trainee pull-up match is Wavering Heart [web:game8.co/games/FF7-Remake/archives/286540] [web:game8.co/games/FF7-Remake/archives/286413]
- The Hard Whack-a-Box course opens on a return visit in Chapter 14 [web:game8.co/games/FF7-Remake/archives/286499]

### Shops & exchange
- A Whack-a-Box run pays one prize, so collecting all of them takes several runs; Moogle Medals can be won without limit [web:game8.co/games/FF7-Remake/archives/286499]
