### M174 Press Turn Battle System
pointers: [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Press Turn] [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Shin Megami Tensei III: Nocturne] [gf:23739 §PRESS TURNS, Ian_Kelley v1.2] [gf:23739 §"FLASHING" PRESS TURN ICONS, Ian_Kelley v1.2]
row: M174
- Each side gets one Press Turn icon for every unit it has in the battle, and an ordinary action spends one [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Press Turn] [gf:23739 §PRESS TURNS, Ian_Kelley v1.2]
- A critical hit or a hit on a weakness does not spend an icon: it turns a whole icon into a flashing half one, and once every icon is flashing the next is spent [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Press Turn] [gf:23739 §PRESS TURNS, Ian_Kelley v1.2] [gf:23739 §"FLASHING" PRESS TURN ICONS, Ian_Kelley v1.2]
- An attack the target dodges or voids costs two icons; one it reflects or absorbs costs every icon left and ends the side's turn [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Press Turn] [gf:23739 §PRESS TURNS, Ian_Kelley v1.2]
- Enemies play by the same rules, so a party that leaves its weaknesses open hands the enemy extra actions [gf:23739 §PRESS TURNS, Ian_Kelley v1.2] [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Press Turn]

## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Hidden
- Completing the Puzzle Boy is needed to complete the Magatama collection, and so to reach Masakados [gf:34200 §What is the Puzzle Boy?, entropic_soul v1.0]

### Upgrades
- Geis adds St +2, Ma +2 and Vi +4 in both guides [gf:34200 §The Prize: Geis Magatama, entropic_soul v1.0] [gf:23739 §GEIS ゲッシュ (LIGHT), Ian_Kelley v1.2]
- A sacrificial fusion's child inherits skills from all three parents and one and a half times the sacrifice's experience [gf:23739 §SACRIFICE FUSIONS, Ian_Kelley v1.2]
- How many skills a fused demon inherits grows with how many its parents know, from 1 at 4 known to 6 at 20–24, the last only by sacrifice; which ones is a weighted random draw [gf:23739 §SKILL INHERITANCE, Ian_Kelley v1.2]
- A Mitama fused into a demon keeps its experience and skills and adds stats [gf:23739 §MITAMA FUSIONS, Ian_Kelley v1.2]

### Shops & exchange
- Elements and Mitama can also be bought at Rag's in Ginza [gf:23739 §SEIREI FUSIONS, Ian_Kelley v1.2] [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Mitama]

## Unverified or contradicted

- **Bosses' extra icons.** M174 said most bosses begin with two icons. Only the Megami Tensei Wiki says so (its Press Turn page, and "more usually denoting a boss" on the Nocturne page); neither guide read does. Cut from the row [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Press Turn]
- **One Magatama at a time.** Ian_Kelley says the Demi-fiend can use only one Magatama at a time; no second source read says so outright. The row says he equips one, not that he can use no other [gf:23739 §MAGATAMA, Ian_Kelley v1.2]
- **24 or 25.** M175 said 25 and then "all 24". The Magatama page and Ian_Kelley count 25 in all, and the Masakados page and Ian_Kelley say the 24 others open the way to Masakados, which settles it; the Masakados page also says it sits outside the equip screen's 25 slots, which would make it a 26th. The row follows the two agreeing readings [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Masakados] [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Magatama] [gf:23739 §MAGATAMA, Ian_Kelley v1.2]
- **Who hands over the sword.** Ian_Kelley has the master of the Jakyo Manor give the Prince's Sword; the wiki's Cathedral of Shadows page has its minister give the Lord's Sword. One place and one sword under two translations; the row names neither [gf:23739 §BONUS DUNGEON: THE BANDO SHRINE, Ian_Kelley v1.2] [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Cathedral of Shadows]
- **Geis's Agility and Luck.** Both guides give +3 each; the wiki's stat box gives +4 each. Left out of the rows [gf:34200 §The Prize: Geis Magatama, entropic_soul v1.0] [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Geis]
- **Geis's other two skills.** entropic_soul lists Life Refill and Mana Refill, as does the wiki; Ian_Kelley calls them Breath Shoes and Chakra Shoes. The table names only the four the three agree on [gf:34200 §The Prize: Geis Magatama, entropic_soul v1.0] [gf:23739 §GEIS ゲッシュ (LIGHT), Ian_Kelley v1.2]
- **Free fusion.** M176 called the Cathedral free; no source read says what a fusion costs. Cut [gf:23739 §FUSION BASICS, Ian_Kelley v1.2]
- **Skills a demon could never learn.** M176 said a bred demon carries abilities it could never learn alone; the wiki says so only of Persona, and Ian_Kelley says instead that each demon may inherit only the skill types it is allowed. The row says the child inherits some of its parents' skills [gf:23739 §HEY, IT WON'T LET ME INHERIT THAT SKILL, Ian_Kelley v1.2]
- **Where the machine stands.** g076 put it in an underground passage; entropic_soul's directions go down a flight of stairs to the third of three rooms, and no second source says where in Asakusa it is. The row says Asakusa [gf:34200 §Location of the Puzzle Boy Game, entropic_soul v1.0]
- **The blue cube.** Ian_Kelley's goal is a spinning blue block; entropic_soul's is an ending square. The row says the goal [gf:23739 §INTERLUDE: THE PUZZLE BOY GAME, Ian_Kelley v1.2] [gf:34200 §What is the Puzzle Boy?, entropic_soul v1.0]
