## Mechanics candidates

The seven existing rows, sourced on 2026-09-16. No new rows: M079–M085 are edited in place.

### M079 Locks (telegraphed attack-break puzzles)
pointers: [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Combat] [web:game8.co/reviews/sea-of-stars/864]
row: M079
- An enemy charging a spell shows a set of locks and a timer; each lock's icon names the damage type that breaks it, such as Sword or Blunt [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Combat] [web:game8.co/reviews/sea-of-stars/864]
- Breaking locks weakens the spell, and breaking all of them cancels it [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Combat]; Game8 describes the locks as conditions that interrupt the move [web:game8.co/reviews/sea-of-stars/864]
- The timer counts the party's turns, and one attack can break several locks, as a boosted attack dealing two damage types does [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Combat] [web:game8.co/reviews/sea-of-stars/864]
- Breaking locks also pays a large number of Combo Points [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Combat]

### M080 Timed hits + Live Mana
pointers: [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Combat] [web:game8.co/reviews/sea-of-stars/864] [web:www.thegamer.com/sea-of-stars-beginner-tips]
row: M080
- Every attack, skill and combo except an ultimate can be timed with a button press for extra power, sometimes extra hits [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Combat] [web:game8.co/reviews/sea-of-stars/864]
- Nearly every enemy attack can be blocked by a timed press, reducing the damage [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Combat] [web:www.thegamer.com/sea-of-stars-beginner-tips]; TheGamer puts both effects at roughly 30 percent [web:www.thegamer.com/sea-of-stars-beginner-tips]
- Regular attacks leave Live Mana on the field, up to three charges [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Combat] [web:game8.co/reviews/sea-of-stars/864]
- A party member absorbs the charges to boost the next action with magic, adding their own element to a regular attack [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Combat] [web:game8.co/reviews/sea-of-stars/864]

### M081 Relics (difficulty as collectible items)
pointers: [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Items] [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Artful Gambit] [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Amulet of Storytelling] [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-amulet-of-storytelling-how-change-difficulty-settings-relics] [web:www.thegamer.com/sea-of-stars-relics-best-most-useful] [web:game8.co/reviews/sea-of-stars/864]
row: M081
- There are no ordinary difficulty levels; relics switched on or off in a Relics menu make the game easier, harder or more accessible [web:www.thegamer.com/sea-of-stars-relics-best-most-useful] [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-amulet-of-storytelling-how-change-difficulty-settings-relics]
- Some come at the start and the rest are found or bought, several at the Brisk secret store for 150 gold each [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-amulet-of-storytelling-how-change-difficulty-settings-relics] [web:www.thegamer.com/sea-of-stars-relics-best-most-useful] [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Items]
- The Amulet of Storytelling fully heals the party after every battle, and at launch also doubled its HP [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Amulet of Storytelling] [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-amulet-of-storytelling-how-change-difficulty-settings-relics] [web:game8.co/reviews/sea-of-stars/864]
- The Artful Gambit cuts HP by 95% and makes enemies invincible while casting, while timed blocks cut damage to 1 and timed hits double their bonus; it sits in a chest at the Throne of The Queen That Was [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Artful Gambit] [web:game8.co/reviews/sea-of-stars/864]
- The achievement "What a technique!" asks for 10 bosses beaten with the Artful Gambit on [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-full-trophy-achievement-list]

### M082 Rainbow Conches → tiered trade-in
pointers: [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Rainbow Conch] [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Rainbow Star] [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-all-rainbow-conch-locations] [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-how-get-true-ending]
row: M082
- There are 60 Rainbow Conches, traded to Mirna at Docarri Village [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Rainbow Conch] [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-all-rainbow-conch-locations] [web:game8.co/reviews/sea-of-stars/864]
- Her rewards come at set totals: Inn Plans at 4, Cornucopia at 11, Shop Plans at 19, Fishing Hut Plans at 22, the Falcon-eyed Parrot at 27, a Poutine recipe at 35, Spa Plans at 39, a Pudding Chômeur recipe at 47 and the Rainbow Star at 60 [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Rainbow Conch]; Game Rant gives the same totals for the four plans, the parrot, the pudding recipe and the star [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-all-rainbow-conch-locations] [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-all-building-plans-locations]
- Bringing all 60 is a step toward the true ending [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-how-get-true-ending]; the wiki says the Rainbow Star, once the first ending is done, lights the left monolith of The Chronophage [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Rainbow Star]

### M083 Mirth (build a town from found plans)
pointers: [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Mirth] [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-all-building-plans-locations] [web:www.thegamer.com/sea-of-stars-relics-best-most-useful]
row: M083
- Giving building plans to the town's builder raises an inn, a shop, a fishing hut and a spa in Mirth [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Mirth] [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-all-building-plans-locations]
- All four plans come from Mirna for Rainbow Conches [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-all-building-plans-locations] [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Rainbow Conch]
- Each building also needs a worker found elsewhere: the merchants in Docarri Village, the Master Fisherman at Mooncradle, the spa's Chi at the Stonemasons Outpost, and an innkeeper [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Mirth] [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-all-building-plans-locations]
- The spa gives each party member one fixed stat boost for 250 gold [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Mirth] [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-all-building-plans-locations]; the fishing hut's master rewards catches of up to 23 species [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Mirth]
- Mirth's buildings sell relics too: the Salient Sails at its equipment shop and the Mithreel Rod from the fishing hut [web:www.thegamer.com/sea-of-stars-relics-best-most-useful] [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-amulet-of-storytelling-how-change-difficulty-settings-relics]

## Unverified or contradicted
- **Timed hits feed resources.** M080 said so, and `docs/lineages.md` quoted it for the timing lineage. The sources give timed hits extra power and extra hits only; MP comes from regular attacks, timed or not. The clause is cut and the node re-quoted [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Combat] [web:game8.co/reviews/sea-of-stars/864]
- **Any character absorbs the orbs.** Kept in substance: the wiki says one party member takes up to three charges [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Combat]
- **The Amulet of Storytelling as a near auto-win.** M081 said so; the sources say it heals after each battle (and once doubled HP), and the row says that [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Amulet of Storytelling] [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-amulet-of-storytelling-how-change-difficulty-settings-relics]
- **Toggleable anytime, and the achievement.** M081 said relics toggle at any time; the sources describe a Relics menu without saying when it may be used, so "anytime" is cut. The Artful Gambit achievement is on Game Rant's list alone and stays here [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-full-trophy-achievement-list] [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-amulet-of-storytelling-how-change-difficulty-settings-relics]
- **How many relics.** TheGamer counts fifteen; the wiki counts 22 after the Dawn of Equinox update. The row gives no count [web:www.thegamer.com/sea-of-stars-relics-best-most-useful] [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Items]
- **No conch is missable.** M082 said so; no source read says it, and it is cut [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Rainbow Conch] [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-all-rainbow-conch-locations]
- **The Rainbow Star as the true ending's requirement.** Game Rant lists the 60 conches as one of several steps; the wiki says the star lights a monolith. The row calls it a step toward the true ending [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-how-get-true-ending] [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Rainbow Star]
- **Plans found through exploration.** M083 said so; both sources put all four plans at Mirna's conch counter, and the row now says that [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-all-building-plans-locations] [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Rainbow Conch]
- **Who builds, and who asks after the innkeeper.** The wiki gives the plans to Bob and names Edgar; Game Rant names Jirard the Constructionist and Edward. The row names neither [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Mirth] [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-all-building-plans-locations]
- **A parrot that reports every remaining collectible on a selected island.** M084 said so; the sources say it gives hints about what is left, and the row says that [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Falcon-eyed Parrot] [web:www.thegamer.com/sea-of-stars-relics-best-most-useful]
- **Light beams, shadows and tides.** M085 named the three; the sources describe light, crystals and runes only, so shadows and tides are cut [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-all-solstice-shrine-locations]
- **Where the shrines' portal opens.** The wiki puts it in the cave near the summit of the Mountain Trail; Game Rant says under the Elder Mist. The row says only that a portal opens [wiki:seaofstars.fandom.com/Solstice Shrines] [web:gamerant.com/sea-of-stars-all-solstice-shrine-locations]
