## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Hidden
- The search has to be learned from Cordell in Baskar Colony; until then pressing Square does nothing [gf:25344 §Baskar Colony, Cyril v2.0]
- Conversation has its own layer: highlighted words can be asked about, and some of those answers are the clues to hidden places [gf:58236 §II. TH' BASICS (THBS), Shotgunnova]
- A Sonar Kit shows undiscovered items, towns and dungeons within the Map Scope's range [gf:22648 §D) HELPFUL ITEMS, Split_Infinity v1.52]

### Upgrades
- Split_Infinity gives each character a ceiling of 24 Personal Skills, and a Medium a ceiling of eight [gf:22648 §1. INVOKE COMMAND, Split_Infinity v1.52] [gf:22648 §3. EQUIP, Split_Infinity v1.52]
- Points can be taken back out of a skill with Reset, or spread automatically by one of five priorities [gf:22648 §1. INVOKE COMMAND, Split_Infinity v1.52]
- Gear teaches 55 of the game's 60 Personal Skills [gf:22648 §LEGEND, Split_Infinity v1.52]

### Shops & exchange
- The World Screen is sold by Roykman, and nowhere else [gf:25344 §Roykman Inventory, Cyril v2.0] [gf:58236 §05) The Sting Pt. II (WK01), Shotgunnova]

## Unverified or contradicted

- **ATT Plus's multipliers.** M190 said ATT Plus scales physical attack 1.5×, 2×, 2.5× and 3× over its four levels. Only Split_Infinity's skill list gives numbers, under a heading too long to cite; Cyril mentions a level-4 ATT Plus without them. Cut from the row [gf:25344 §The Abyss, Cyril v2.0]
- **Two dozen skills at once.** M190 said up to roughly two dozen active per character. Split_Infinity's 24 has no second source. Cut [gf:22648 §1. INVOKE COMMAND, Split_Infinity v1.52]
- **Signposts as clues.** M191 said clues come from NPCs or signposts. Split_Infinity says signposts give clues, but in its uncitable tutorial; Shotgunnova says the search turns signposts up without saying they give clues. The row says clues come from people [gf:58236 §II. TH' BASICS (THBS), Shotgunnova]
- **Coordinates on the Map Scope.** M191 said the Map Scope puts the coordinates on screen. Split_Infinity says it projects the map and Shotgunnova that it adds more views; neither mentions coordinates. Cut [gf:22648 §D) HELPFUL ITEMS, Split_Infinity v1.52] [gf:58236 §05) The Sting Pt. II (WK01), Shotgunnova]
- **Vague clues.** M191's notes said WA3's clues are often so vague that discovery turns to frustration. One walkthrough calls one clue vague; the notes now name the risk without a frequency [gf:58236 §LOCATING NEXT DUNGEON, Shotgunnova]
- **The EX File Key and New Game+.** g079 said the key unlocks New Game+. The wiki and Cyril say the 15 keys are spent on a menu of extras once the game is finished, and neither ties this key to a new game. The row says extras [wiki:wildarms.fandom.com/Ex. File] [gf:25344 §EX File Keys, Cyril v2.0]
- **The LVL Apple.** g079's table called it a permanent stat boost; both sources say it raises the level by one [gf:22648 §A) CONSUMABLE ITEMS, Split_Infinity v1.52] [gf:25344 §LVL Apple, Cyril v2.0]
- **Puzzle 13's grade.** Split_Infinity rates it Hard; Cyril gives it 3 of 5, the Medium band. The table gives no grade [gf:22648 §XXI) MILLENIUM PUZZLES, Split_Infinity v1.52] [gf:25344 §Millennium Puzzle #13, Cyril v2.0]
- **When the puzzles open.** Split_Infinity says after the Fortune Gear dungeon; Cyril says only to chat with Simon. The row does not say when [gf:22648 §XXI) MILLENIUM PUZZLES, Split_Infinity v1.52]
