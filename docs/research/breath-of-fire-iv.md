### M142 Masters (Apprenticeship) System
pointers: [wiki:bof.fandom.com/Master System] [wiki:bof.fandom.com/List of Breath of Fire IV Masters] [gf:9833 §9. Masters, Billy_Lee v4.30]
row: M142
- Twelve masters can be found in the game [wiki:bof.fandom.com/List of Breath of Fire IV Masters] [gf:9833 §9. Masters, Billy_Lee v4.30]
- Apprenticing to one alters how a character's stats grow on level up, up or down, master by master [wiki:bof.fandom.com/Master System] [gf:9833 §9. Masters, Billy_Lee v4.30]
- Each master also grants a will, a passive effect — Greed raises the zenny a battle pays, Wild raises damage and lowers accuracy [wiki:bof.fandom.com/Master System] [gf:9833 §9. Masters, Billy_Lee v4.30]
- Skills are taught for meeting themed conditions rather than at fixed levels: damage in a single combo, hours played, an item acquired [wiki:bof.fandom.com/Master System] [gf:9833 §9. Masters, Billy_Lee v4.30]
- Worked conditions: Una's Blitz at 10,000 damage in one combo; Stole's Coward's Way at 120 different items carried; the Abbess's Celerity at 100 different monsters fought; Kahn's Flex at 600 encounters; Kryrik's Disembowel at a 70-hit combo [gf:9833 §9. Masters, Billy_Lee v4.30] [wiki:bof.fandom.com/List of Breath of Fire IV Masters]
- Masters gate each other and other systems: Gyosil needs 3,000 fishing points, Lyta needs someone apprenticed under Gyosil, Bunyan needs every other master [gf:9833 §9. Masters, Billy_Lee v4.30] [wiki:bof.fandom.com/List of Breath of Fire IV Masters]
- Njomo, found at a "?" spot, teaches faerie skills by the village's population — FaerieAttack at 8 faeries, War Shout at 16 [gf:9833 §9. Masters, Billy_Lee v4.30] [gf:9833 §7-D-a: Faerie Shopping Lists, Billy_Lee v4.30]
- The camp's master menu lets the party switch masters without travelling to them [wiki:bof.fandom.com/Master System]

### M143 Dragon Gems & Game-Point Form Upgrades
pointers: [wiki:bof.fandom.com/Dragon Form System] [gf:9833 §12-D: Evolving Dragons, Billy_Lee v4.30] [gf:9928 §D. Mini-Game Madness, Raiu v1.5] [gf:9928 §C. Infini, Raiu v1.5]
row: M143
- Ryu and Fou-Lu transform by selecting a Dragon Gem, becoming a dragon-human hybrid with new abilities and stats [wiki:bof.fandom.com/Dragon Form System] [gf:9928 §2. Wyvern, Raiu v1.5]
- The gems are found in the world, some of them at unmarked "?" areas [gf:9928 §2. Wyvern, Raiu v1.5] [gf:9928 §3. Behemoth, Raiu v1.5] [wiki:bof.fandom.com/Ni Ryong]
- Forms are permanently upgraded by spending Game Points, and a form that can upgrade can only do it once [wiki:bof.fandom.com/Dragon Form System] [gf:9833 §12-D: Evolving Dragons, Billy_Lee v4.30]
- The thresholds are 10,000, 20,000, 35,000 and 50,000 Game Points; at each one the next elemental dragon selected upgrades automatically [gf:9833 §12-D: Evolving Dragons, Billy_Lee v4.30] [wiki:bof.fandom.com/Dragon Form System]
- Named upgrades: Wyvern to Weyr, Behemoth to Mammoth, Myrmidon to Knight, Mutant to Punk, Serpent to Peist [gf:9833 §12-D: Evolving Dragons, Billy_Lee v4.30]
- Game Points come from minigames and from advancing the story, and a minigame's first completion pays ten times what a replay does [gf:9833 §3. Basics, Billy_Lee v4.30] [gf:9928 §D. Mini-Game Madness, Raiu v1.5]
- Points are LOST for hitting people in the field with Ryu's sword, Ershin's bash or Ursula's gun [gf:9833 §3. Basics, Billy_Lee v4.30] [gf:9928 §D. Mini-Game Madness, Raiu v1.5]
- Fishing points are a separate currency and cannot be spent on dragons [gf:9928 §D. Mini-Game Madness, Raiu v1.5]
- Seven dragon gods are gathered separately as Evocation summons; one is given by the story and the rest are found by talking to each dragon's avatar [gf:9928 §C. Infini, Raiu v1.5] [wiki:bof.fandom.com/Endless]
- One of them, the Mud Dragon, is picked up at the "?" spot north of the Dam; another, the Rock Dragon, only after telling a man by the river that dragons can look like grass and rocks [wiki:bof.fandom.com/Ni Ryong] [gf:9928 §C. Infini, Raiu v1.5]

### M144 Learn-and-Share Skills (Defend to steal, Aurum to transfer)
pointers: [gf:9833 §3. Basics, Billy_Lee v4.30] [gf:9877 §Skills, Dalez v1.05] [wiki:bof.fandom.com/Aurum]
row: M144
- Guarding is what learns a skill: be defending when the enemy performs it, with no need to pick a target as Breath of Fire III's Examine did [gf:9833 §3. Basics, Billy_Lee v4.30] [gf:9877 §Combat, Dalez v1.05]
- Not every skill can be learned, and some enemies only use theirs in certain situations [gf:9877 §Skills, Dalez v1.05] [gf:9833 §3. Basics, Billy_Lee v4.30]
- Checking an enemy shows which of its skills are learnable: blue can be learned, red cannot, black is already learned or not a skill [gf:9833 §3. Basics, Billy_Lee v4.30]
- A learned skill is moved to another character through the Skill option in camp, which costs an Aurum [gf:9877 §Skills, Dalez v1.05] [wiki:bof.fandom.com/Aurum]
- An Aurum removes a skill from a character to the Skill Scroll, or puts one back [wiki:bof.fandom.com/Aurum]
- Some of Ryu's dragon-form attacks can be cast on a party member to teach them the skill [gf:9833 §3. Basics, Billy_Lee v4.30]
- Aurum is also traded as a commodity at the Faerie Village [wiki:bof.fandom.com/Aurum] [gf:9833 §7. Fairy Village, Billy_Lee v4.30]

### Upgrades

## Unverified or contradicted

- **Fish powering the dragons.** M143's note said fishing, crane games and raft runs "literally power up your dragon". Raiu states outright that fishing points are separate from Game Points and cannot be spent on dragons. The note is rewritten [gf:9928 §D. Mini-Game Madness, Raiu v1.5]
- **The named fish payouts.** g060's table gave Bass 500 HP, Dorado 800 HP to all and Sturgeon reviving the party, plus zenny values by species. All three fish exist, but no source read here gives those effects or prices; the wiki says only that fish effects run from healing HP and AP to magical attacks. The table now carries the Manillo trades and the fishing-point tiers instead [wiki:bof.fandom.com/Fishing System] [gf:9833 §Type Fish Points, Billy_Lee v4.30]
- **Faerie aptitudes.** g061 said newborn faeries carry hunt, build, trade and scholar aptitudes. Those are Breath of Fire III's job names; in IV a faerie has an SL rating and a personality that affect a shop's stock and gallery, and any faerie can take any job [wiki:bof.fandom.com/Faerie Village]
- **Where War Shout comes from.** g061 credited the Music shop and Troops with unlocking War Shout. The skill is taught by the master Njomo at 16 faeries; Troops plus a Music shop is what makes it work [gf:9833 §9. Masters, Billy_Lee v4.30] [wiki:bof.fandom.com/Faerie Village]
- **"Encounter 100 monsters".** M142's note gave this as a master's condition. Kahn's conditions count encounters (300 to 600); the Abbess's count how many different kinds of monster have been fought (70, 85, 100). The note now names the right one [gf:9833 §9. Masters, Billy_Lee v4.30]
- **Whether apprenticing is needed to learn a master's skills.** The wiki says a player can unlock every one of a master's skills without ever apprenticing under them; no guide read here says either way. The row states the conditions and leaves the question out [wiki:bof.fandom.com/Master System]
- **Seven summons "found purely by exploring".** One of the seven, Rainstorm, is given by the story; the other six are found [gf:9928 §C. Infini, Raiu v1.5]
