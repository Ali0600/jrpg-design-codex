### M125 Item Creation (a dozen crafting hobbies)
pointers: [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Item Creation] [gf:43189 §2) Detailed Skill Point, A_I_e_x] [gf:4645 §Those impossible skills, Aya_Brea v2.7]
row: M125
- Skills bought with skill points open Item Creation specialties, which turn materials into items and can fail [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Item Creation] [gf:43189 §2) Detailed Skill Point, A_I_e_x]
- The specialties include Cooking, Compounding, Metalwork, Authoring, Alchemy, Machinery, Reproduction, Identify and Survival [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Item Creation] [gf:43189 §2) Detailed Skill Point, A_I_e_x]
- Talents decide success, and a character without one can learn it by practising, as cooking until Sense of Taste appears [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Item Creation] [gf:43189 §(preamble), A_I_e_x] [gf:4645 §Those impossible skills, Aya_Brea v2.7]

### M126 Private Actions (split up in towns)
pointers: [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Private Action] [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Star Ocean: The Second Story] [gf:43189 §Jewelry Store Fairy Tear, A_I_e_x]
row: M126
- Starting a Private Action outside a town splits up the party inside it, and talking to them can trigger scenes [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Private Action] [gf:43189 §Jewelry Store Fairy Tear, A_I_e_x]
- Choices in those scenes change the relationship values between characters, which decide the endings [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Private Action] [gf:43189 §Jewelry Store Fairy Tear, A_I_e_x]
- There are more than 80 endings: one for each character alone and one for each possible pair [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Star Ocean: The Second Story] [gf:43189 §Jewelry Store Fairy Tear, A_I_e_x]

### M127 Super Specialties (party-wide combined skills)
pointers: [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Specialty] [gf:43189 §Secret Charity / Fake Gal, A_I_e_x] [gf:4645 §Those impossible skills, Aya_Brea v2.7]
row: M127
- Super Specialties are unlocked by specialties held across the party, not by one character [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Specialty] [gf:43189 §Secret Charity / Fake Gal, A_I_e_x]
- They include Master Chef, Orchestra, Publishing, Blacksmith and Reverse Side [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Specialty] [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Item Creation] [gf:43189 §2) Detailed Skill Point, A_I_e_x]
- Orchestra plays the party's songs and greatly raises the chance of success at item creation and of learning a talent [gf:43189 §2) Detailed Skill Point, A_I_e_x] [gf:4645 §Those impossible skills, Aya_Brea v2.7]

## Unverified or contradicted

- **Bunny Call as a mount.** M127 said Bunny Call summons a giant rabbit to ride over mountains. The wiki lists Bunny Call among the Super Specialties by name only, and neither GameFAQs guide mentions it (every "Bunny" hit is the Bunny Shoes), so the row drops it, and its loop and notes are rewritten [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Specialty] [gf:43189 §2) Detailed Skill Point, A_I_e_x] [gf:4645 §Those impossible skills, Aya_Brea v2.7]
- **Painting, Music and Pickpocketing as crafts.** M125 named them among the creation hobbies. The wiki's list of creation specialties for this game has no Music or Pickpocket (they are ordinary specialties), and the walkthrough's creation list matches the wiki's, so the row uses that list [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Item Creation] [wiki:starocean.fandom.com/Specialty] [gf:43189 §2) Detailed Skill Point, A_I_e_x]
- **"Famously breaks the game" and endings from battles.** Neither is in both sources; the notes' balance warning is kept as advice without the claim, and battles fought together raising relationships (A_I_e_x only) is left out of M126 [gf:43189 §Jewelry Store Fairy Tear, A_I_e_x]
