## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Hidden
- Each Gunner's Gauntlet prize comes once, a score that skips a level forfeits that level's prize, and the best score carries into New Game Plus [web:jegged.com/Games/Final-Fantasy-X-2/Side-Quests/Gunners-Gauntlet.html]
- The three elite Sphere Break players exist only in the International and HD Remaster releases [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Sphere Break]
- Ragnarok can also be won in the Farplane Cup [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Sphere Break] [web:jegged.com/Games/Final-Fantasy-X-2/Accessories/]

### Upgrades
- Reaching publicity level 3 with a company opens its minigame, Gull Force Live for Open Air and Feed the Monkey for Argent [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Publicity Campaign]
- Switching companies halves the points earned for the first one [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Publicity Campaign]
- The campaign needs 400 points to finish [web:jegged.com/Games/Final-Fantasy-X-2/Side-Quests/Publicity-and-Marriage-Campaigns.html]

### Shops & exchange
- Level 5 also sells the Sword Tome (Open Air) and Black Tome (Argent) at 80,000 credits, and buying each company's grid adds a 100,000-credit tome, the Bushido Tome or the White Tome [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Publicity Campaign]

## Unverified or contradicted
- **The coin-trait grids.** The wiki says the White Signet and Treasure Hunt are won at random from item-trait combos; Jegged gives the combo as the condition without mentioning chance [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Sphere Break Tournament] [web:jegged.com/Games/Final-Fantasy-X-2/Garment-Grids/]
