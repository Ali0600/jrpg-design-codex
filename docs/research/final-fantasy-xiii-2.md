## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Hidden
- The Lucky Coin comes from winning 7,777 coins in total at the slots, across any number of sittings [gf:63762 §3.11.9. Lucky Coin, ching_chong_7AD v1.05]
- The Chaos Crystal from the exchange desk is needed for the Odinblade and Odinbolt paradox weapons [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Serendipity (Final Fantasy XIII-2)]

### Upgrades
- SunsThirdStone names the discount skill Bargain Hunter and ching_chong_7AD names it Haggler; both put the discounted price at 7,500 [gf:63873 §A05: SERENDIPITY, SunsThirdStone v1.0.1] [gf:63762 §3.8.16. Setzer's Dice, ching_chong_7AD v1.05]

### Shops & exchange
- Casino coins are bought for gil at the exchange desk at ten gil a coin [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Serendipity (Final Fantasy XIII-2)]
- The walkthrough gives the same rate, 100,000 gil for the 10,000 chips a Wild Artefact costs, and lists the rest of the shop, from a Shuffle at 50 coins to an Elixir at 30,000, with a Ribbon at 10,000 and Just 1 gil! at 9,800 [gf:63553 §WLK14 AF??? Serendipity, Split_Infinity]

## Unverified or contradicted
- **The Chaos Crystal's price.** The wiki lists 10,000 coins; the Fragment Guide prints 1,000 while giving 7,500 as the discounted price [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Serendipity (Final Fantasy XIII-2)] [gf:63762 §3.1.1. Chaos Crystal, ching_chong_7AD v1.05]. The walkthrough read on 2026-09-15 lists it at 10,000, siding with the wiki [gf:63553 §WLK14 AF??? Serendipity, Split_Infinity]
- **What Haggler does.** ching_chong_7AD's Haggler and SunsThirdStone's Bargain Hunter both cut the exchange price to 7,500; the walkthrough describes Haggler as a skill for selling items for more gil [gf:63762 §3.8.16. Setzer's Dice, ching_chong_7AD v1.05] [gf:63873 §A05: SERENDIPITY, SunsThirdStone v1.0.1] [gf:63553 §WLK14 AF??? Serendipity, Split_Infinity]
- **The Priceless Gift.** The wiki lists it at the exchange desk for 10,000 coins; the Fragment Guide says it is earned by buying Setzer's Dice, the Chaos Crystal and Just 1 Gil [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Serendipity (Final Fantasy XIII-2)] [gf:63762 §3.11.10. Priceless Gift, ching_chong_7AD v1.05]. The walkthrough's list of the shop has no Priceless Gift in it [gf:63553 §WLK14 AF??? Serendipity, Split_Infinity]
