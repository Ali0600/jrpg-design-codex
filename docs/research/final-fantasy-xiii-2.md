## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Hidden
- The Lucky Coin comes from winning 7,777 coins in total at the slots, across any number of sittings [gf:63762 §3.11.9. Lucky Coin, ching_chong_7AD v1.05]
- The Chaos Crystal from the exchange desk is needed for the Odinblade and Odinbolt paradox weapons [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Serendipity (Final Fantasy XIII-2)]

### Upgrades
- SunsThirdStone names the discount skill Bargain Hunter and ching_chong_7AD names it Haggler; both put the discounted price at 7,500 [gf:63873 §A05: SERENDIPITY, SunsThirdStone v1.0.1] [gf:63762 §3.8.16. Setzer's Dice, ching_chong_7AD v1.05]

### Shops & exchange
- Casino coins are bought for gil at the exchange desk at ten gil a coin [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Serendipity (Final Fantasy XIII-2)]

## Unverified or contradicted
- **The Chaos Crystal's price.** The wiki lists 10,000 coins; the Fragment Guide prints 1,000 while giving 7,500 as the discounted price [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Serendipity (Final Fantasy XIII-2)] [gf:63762 §3.1.1. Chaos Crystal, ching_chong_7AD v1.05]
- **The Priceless Gift.** The wiki lists it at the exchange desk for 10,000 coins; the Fragment Guide says it is earned by buying Setzer's Dice, the Chaos Crystal and Just 1 Gil [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Serendipity (Final Fantasy XIII-2)] [gf:63762 §3.11.10. Priceless Gift, ching_chong_7AD v1.05]
