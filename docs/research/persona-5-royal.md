## Mechanics candidates

The seven existing rows, sourced on 2026-09-16. No new rows: M044–M050 are edited in place.

### M044 Confidants (relationships ARE upgrades)
pointers: [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Confidant] [gf:78335 §12. Hanged Man - Munehisa Iwai, Road_to_Dawn v0.95] [gf:78335 §0. Fool - Igor, Road_to_Dawn v0.95]
row: M044
- Each confidant is tied to an arcana; ranking one up gives newly fused personas of that arcana bonus experience and shortens their resistance training in the Velvet Room, and unlocks new abilities [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Confidant]
- The guide lists 23 confidants, Igor to Maruki, with their rank unlocks: Iwai's Starter, Camo and Expert Customization and a Discount, and Takemi's Discount [gf:78335 §12. Hanged Man - Munehisa Iwai, Road_to_Dawn v0.95] [gf:78335 §13. Death - Tae Takemi, Road_to_Dawn v0.95]
- Some ranks unlock personas for fusion, such as Vishnu from the Fool and Futsunushi from the Magician [gf:78335 §0. Fool - Igor, Road_to_Dawn v0.95] [gf:78335 §1. Magician - Morgana, Road_to_Dawn v0.95]

### M045 Calendar / time economy
pointers: [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Calendar] [gf:80350 §(preamble), Personabot345]
row: M045
- The calendar decides when activities are available, some only at a certain time of day, and sets the deadlines that must be met [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Calendar]
- A day-by-day plan gives each date a day slot and a night slot — a palace, darts, a job at the flower shop, a confidant hangout, reading — one activity each [gf:80350 §(preamble), Personabot345]

### M046 Social stats gating content
pointers: [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Social Stats] [gf:78335 §2. Priestess - Makoto Niijima, Road_to_Dawn v0.95] [gf:78335 §3. Empress - Haru Okumura, Road_to_Dawn v0.95]
row: M046
- There are five social stats — Knowledge, Guts, Proficiency, Kindness and Charm — and raising them lets the protagonist start or resume certain confidants and work certain part-time jobs [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Social Stats]
- Each stat needs different effort: Proficiency takes the fewest points to max, Knowledge the most [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Social Stats]
- Confidant ranks name their requirements: Makoto's first rank needs Knowledge at level 3, her sixth Charm at level 5, and Haru's second and fourth Proficiency at level 5 [gf:78335 §2. Priestess - Makoto Niijima, Road_to_Dawn v0.95] [gf:78335 §3. Empress - Haru Okumura, Road_to_Dawn v0.95]

### M047 Persona fusion + compendium
pointers: [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Compendium] [gf:78384 §Persona Compendium PCRS, Paltheos v1.0] [gf:78384 §Skill Inheritance GUSI, Paltheos v1.0]
row: M047
- The compendium registers personas and lets any registered one be summoned again for money, at a cost scaling with its level, stats and skills [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Compendium] [gf:78384 §Persona Compendium PCRS, Paltheos v1.0]
- Filling it discounts that cost: 90% at 25% complete, 85% at 50%, 75% at 75% and half price at 100%; DLC personas do not count [gf:78384 §Persona Compendium PCRS, Paltheos v1.0]
- Fused personas inherit skills from their components within limits set by each persona's hidden inheritance type — a Fire type cannot inherit Ice skills, for one [gf:78384 §Skill Inheritance GUSI, Paltheos v1.0]

### M048 Will Seeds (dungeon exploration collectibles)
pointers: [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Will Seed] [gf:78212 §Will Seed Upgrades, marendarade v1.1.3]
row: M048
- Each palace holds three Will Seeds, red, green and blue; collected together they combine into a crystal accessory [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Will Seed]
- Once the palace has collapsed, showing Jose all three has him upgrade the crystal into a ring with extra effects; a missed seed can be bought from him for 400 flowers [gf:78212 §Will Seed Upgrades, marendarade v1.1.3] [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Will Seed]
- Some palaces set a Will Seed mini-boss in the way [gf:78212 §Museum Palace, marendarade v1.1.3]

### M049 Mementos + request board
pointers: [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Mementos] [gf:78212 §Requests, marendarade v1.1.3] [gf:78212 §Stamp Trading, marendarade v1.1.3]
row: M049
- Requests unlock extra mini-bosses in Mementos; most arrive as phone messages naming a target, and a confidant's request must be cleared before that confidant ranks up [gf:78212 §Requests, marendarade v1.1.3] [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Mementos]
- Mementos deepens over the calendar, new paths opening on set dates [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Mementos]
- Royal adds Jose, whose shop takes flowers collected while driving, which reset on leaving, and stamps found on every floor [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Mementos] [gf:78212 §Flower Trading, marendarade v1.1.3]
- Each stamp step raises battle EXP or battle money by 10%, up to +100%, or items per search by one, up to +10 [gf:78212 §Stamp Trading, marendarade v1.1.3]

### M050 Daily activities that upgrade combat
pointers: [gf:78212 §Darts, marendarade v1.1.3] [gf:78212 §Jazz Club, marendarade v1.1.3] [gf:78212 §Gym, marendarade v1.1.3] [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Darts] [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Jazz Jin] [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Batting Cage]
row: M050
- Darts raises the Baton Pass rank of the partner and the protagonist, each member ranked individually; billiards raises the Technical rank the party shares [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Darts] [gf:78212 §Darts, marendarade v1.1.3]
- The jazz club raises an invited member's stats by weekday drink, and its Sunday drink teaches a skill, Tetrakarn and Matarukaja among them [gf:78212 §Jazz Club, marendarade v1.1.3] [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Jazz Jin]
- The gym, unlocked at Chariot rank 5, adds HP +3 and SP +2 a visit at first, rising to HP +9 and SP +7 [gf:78212 §Gym, marendarade v1.1.3]
- The batting cage pays prizes for home runs and clean sessions [gf:78212 §Batting Center, marendarade v1.1.3] [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Batting Cage]

## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Hidden
- Only the phone's latest 50 messages are kept, so a request can be lost for good by not reading it in time [gf:78212 §Requests, marendarade v1.1.3]

### Upgrades
- Room workouts at home add HP +5 a session at first, rising to +8 [gf:78212 §Room Workout (Royal), marendarade v1.1.3]

### Shops & exchange
- Jose's flower shop sells status vials, 30 to 70 flowers each [gf:78212 §Flower Trading, marendarade v1.1.3]

## Unverified or contradicted
- **Other confidant abilities.** M044 named the politician's negotiation, Baton Pass and follow-ups from party members, and evolved personas at max rank; this pass found them on the wiki alone, and cut them from the row [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Confidant]
- **Social stats gating dialogue and story choices.** M046 claimed it; the pages read show confidants and jobs gated, not dialogue or story, and the clause was dropped [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Social Stats] [gf:78335 §2. Priestess - Makoto Niijima, Road_to_Dawn v0.95]
- **Will Seeds in optional rooms off the critical path.** M048 said so; neither source places them, and the clause was dropped [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Will Seed] [gf:78212 §Will Seed Upgrades, marendarade v1.1.3]
- **A procedurally shifting Mementos.** M049 said so; the sources describe paths opening by date, not procedural layouts, and the word was dropped [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Mementos]
- **What the batting cage pays.** The wiki gives a Proficiency note per session plus prizes; marendarade gives accessories in the daytime prize mode. M050 says only prizes, where both agree [wiki:megamitensei.fandom.com/Batting Cage] [gf:78212 §Batting Center, marendarade v1.1.3]
- **Cooking.** M050 said cooking makes recovery items; this pass did not read a source for it, and the clause was dropped [gf:78212 §Darts, marendarade v1.1.3]
