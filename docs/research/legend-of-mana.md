### M109 Land Make (you place the world yourself)
pointers: [wiki:mana.fandom.com/Legend of Mana] [wiki:mana.fandom.com/Artifact] [gf:77187 §Game Features and Mechanics Summary, zykeeper v2.3] [gf:20193 §II. The FAQ, Myst_Erik_Ery v1.05]
row: M109
- The world map is a grid of empty plots, 36 in all, and an Artifact placed on a plot becomes a Land [wiki:mana.fandom.com/Legend of Mana] [gf:20193 §II. The FAQ, Myst_Erik_Ery v1.05]
- Artifacts come from people and as quest rewards; the first, Colorblocks, builds the town of Domina [wiki:mana.fandom.com/Artifact] [gf:77187 §Game Features and Mechanics Summary, zykeeper v2.3] [gf:7933 §HOME, Nemesis v1.01]
- Every Land holds at least one event, and an Artifact can be placed only next to a Land that has been cleared [wiki:mana.fandom.com/Legend of Mana] [gf:20193 §II. The FAQ, Myst_Erik_Ery v1.05]
- Where a Land goes changes its elemental mana values, which shape what the Home orchard grows, and the placement guide builds its map for shop levels, rare pets and rare materials [wiki:mana.fandom.com/Legend of Mana] [gf:77187 §zykeeper's Quests Order & Land Placement, zykeeper v2.3]

### M110 Home workshop suite (a house of systems)
pointers: [wiki:mana.fandom.com/Legend of Mana] [gf:77187 §Game Features and Mechanics Summary, zykeeper v2.3] [gf:7933 §15. How to Blacksmith, Nemesis v1.01] [gf:7933 §MONSTER CORRAL, Nemesis v1.01]
row: M110
- The hero's home holds a smithy, an instrument workshop, a golem workshop, a monster corral and an orchard, each opened by its own quest [wiki:mana.fandom.com/Legend of Mana] [gf:77187 §Game Features and Mechanics Summary, zykeeper v2.3]
- The smithy forges and tempers equipment, tempering with items raising or lowering its power or adding effects [wiki:mana.fandom.com/Legend of Mana] [gf:7933 §15. How to Blacksmith, Nemesis v1.01]
- Instruments are crafted in their own workshop and cast spells in battle [wiki:mana.fandom.com/Legend of Mana] [gf:77187 §Game Features and Mechanics Summary, zykeeper v2.3] [gf:7933 §9. Magic / Instrument / Mana List, Nemesis v1.01]
- Golems are built from weapons and armor, and their attacks come from logic blocks made in a jar [wiki:mana.fandom.com/Legend of Mana] [gf:7933 §GOLEM GO MAKE 'EM, Nemesis v1.01] [gf:77187 §Game Features and Mechanics Summary, zykeeper v2.3]
- The corral raises pets hatched from eggs found in the world, and the orchard's tree grows produce from the seeds fed to it [wiki:mana.fandom.com/Legend of Mana] [gf:7933 §MONSTER CORRAL, Nemesis v1.01] [gf:7933 §MANA ORCHARDS, Nemesis v1.01] [gf:77187 §Game Features and Mechanics Summary, zykeeper v2.3]

### M111 Non-linear events (a web of self-contained stories)
pointers: [wiki:mana.fandom.com/Legend of Mana] [gf:7933 §HOME, Nemesis v1.01] [gf:20193 §II. The FAQ, Myst_Erik_Ery v1.05] [gf:77187 §Introduction, zykeeper v2.3]
row: M111
- The game is built from self-contained events rather than one strong main plot [wiki:mana.fandom.com/Legend of Mana] [gf:77187 §Introduction, zykeeper v2.3]
- Events live in the Lands the player places and start when the player visits places and talks to people [wiki:mana.fandom.com/Legend of Mana] [gf:20193 §II. The FAQ, Myst_Erik_Ery v1.05] [gf:7933 §HOME, Nemesis v1.01]

## Unverified or contradicted

- **68 events.** The wiki counts 67 quests plus the final one. Nemesis's table of contents lists 70 sections, including the opening at Home, and no second source gives a count, so M111 drops the number from its name and text [wiki:mana.fandom.com/Legend of Mana] [gf:7933 §HOME, Nemesis v1.01]
- **No quest log.** M111 said there is no central quest log. zykeeper describes the Cactus Diary, which records every quest finished, so the claim is cut [gf:77187 §Game Features and Mechanics Summary, zykeeper v2.3]
- **Three arcs and "finish any branch to end the game".** Both are on the wiki alone; the AF/Event FAQ names a Jumi arc without counting arcs [wiki:mana.fandom.com/Legend of Mana] [gf:20193 §Mailbox = You begin with it, Myst_Erik_Ery v1.05]
- **Plunging materials to imbue hidden essences, and fruit that feeds everything.** Neither source read describes tempering that way, and the produce is shown feeding pets only, so M110 says tempering and seeds [wiki:mana.fandom.com/Legend of Mana] [gf:7933 §15. How to Blacksmith, Nemesis v1.01]
