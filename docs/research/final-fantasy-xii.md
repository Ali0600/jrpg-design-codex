### M206 The Hunts (a bounty board as a content spine)
pointers: [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Hunt (Final Fantasy XII)] [gf:42270 §CONGRATULATIONS! YOU ARE VICTORIOUS, Split_Infinity v012] [gf:42270 §THE ROYAL CITY OF RABANASTRE town, Split_Infinity v012]
row: M206
- There are 45 marks, posted as bills on notice boards and opened as the story advances [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Hunt (Final Fantasy XII)] [gf:42270 §CONGRATULATIONS! YOU ARE VICTORIOUS, Split_Infinity v012]
- A hunt is accepted from the petitioner the bill names, the mark tracked down and killed, and the reward collected from the petitioner [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Hunt (Final Fantasy XII)] [gf:42270 §CONGRATULATIONS! YOU ARE VICTORIOUS, Split_Infinity v012]
- Montblanc offers Elite Marks as well [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Hunt (Final Fantasy XII)] [gf:42270 §THE ROYAL CITY OF RABANASTRE town, Split_Infinity v012]
- Rewards include License Points, equipment and rare loot, and many hunts are hard for the point at which they appear [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Hunt (Final Fantasy XII)] [gf:42270 §CONGRATULATIONS! YOU ARE VICTORIOUS, Split_Infinity v012]

### M207 License Board
pointers: [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/License Board] [gf:42270 §The Ways of Strength, Split_Infinity v012]
row: M207
- Every spell, technick, augment and piece of equipment is a square on a board, bought with License Points earned by defeating enemies [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/License Board] [gf:42270 §The Ways of Strength, Split_Infinity v012]
- In the original release the board is the same for every character; the Zodiac versions give each job its own [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/License Board] [gf:42270 §The Ways of Strength, Split_Infinity v012]

### M208 Loot -> Bazaar economy
pointers: [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Bazaar (Final Fantasy XII)] [gf:45805 §UNLOCKING BAZAAR GOODS (unlo), FeuFeu v1.6] [gf:42270 §LITTLE TIPS AND TRICKS, Split_Infinity v012]
row: M208
- Selling enough of particular loot adds a bazaar good for sale, from discounted bundles to rare equipment sold nowhere else [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Bazaar (Final Fantasy XII)] [gf:42270 §LITTLE TIPS AND TRICKS, Split_Infinity v012] [gf:45805 §UNLOCKING BAZAAR GOODS (unlo), FeuFeu v1.6]
- It does not matter which shop the loot is sold in: the bazaar is the same in every shop [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Bazaar (Final Fantasy XII)] [gf:45805 §UNLOCKING BAZAAR GOODS (unlo), FeuFeu v1.6]
- Some ultimate weapons come only from the bazaar, the greatsword Tournesol among them [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Bazaar (Final Fantasy XII)] [gf:45805 §UNLOCKING BAZAAR GOODS (unlo), FeuFeu v1.6]

## Exploration & upgrade facts

### Hidden
- The five bottles' messages spell out "underground bridge to nowhere", where Gilgamesh waits with the Matamune [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Fishing (Final Fantasy XII)] [web:jegged.com/Games/Final-Fantasy-XII/Side-Quests/Fishing.html]

### Upgrades
- Each area's perfect-score prize odds are published on the wiki; the Cactoid Compact and Commendation each take a 10% share until caught [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Fishing (Final Fantasy XII)]

### Shops & exchange
- Ada buys fish for more than the shops, and takes 99 Empty Bottles off the player's hands [wiki:finalfantasy.fandom.com/Fishing (Final Fantasy XII)]
